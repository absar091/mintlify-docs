# Your Campus. Your Events. Completely Reengineered.

Welcome to the official documentation for **UniTix** (`unitix.aarx.online`), the next-generation event management, digital ticketing, and financial reconciliation ecosystem designed from the ground up for university societies, campus organizers, and student attendees.

Traditional campus ticketing relies on disorganized WhatsApp spreadsheets, cash boxes, paper sign-up sheets, and unverified Google Forms—leading to rampant gate crashing, financial discrepancies, and scalping. UniTix replaces this outdated infrastructure with a centralized, serverless, **Zero-Trust Ticketing Architecture**.

---

## The Four Core Pillars of the UniTix Ecosystem

### 1. 🛍️ The Student Marketplace
A centralized, high-converting discovery portal where students across university campuses can browse upcoming workshops, cultural festivals, tech symposiums, and sports tournaments.
- **Smart Filtering:** Filter events by category, specific university, verified society, or date horizon.
- **Real-Time Inventory Badges:** Dynamic visual cues indicating *On Sale*, *Selling Fast (`>80% capacity`)*, *Sold Out*, and *Sales Closed*.
- **Localized Payment Gateways:** Seamless checkout supporting both instant free reservations and manual bank verification (`JazzCash`, `EasyPaisa`, `IBFT`) with screenshot receipt auditing.

### 2. 📱 The Gate Scanner & QR Revocation Engine
A progressive web application (`unitix.aarx.online/scan`) turning any mobile smartphone camera into an enterprise-grade venue entrance scanner.
- **Cryptographic JWT Tokens:** Every digital pass contains a signed JSON Web Token (`qrToken`) verifying attendee identity, roll number, and tier.
- **Zero-Screenshot Fraud Protection:** When scanned at the gate, tickets instantly flip to `Used`. Attempting to scan a saved screenshot five minutes later triggers a loud double-scan security alarm.
- **Instant QR Revocation:** If a ticket is transferred or refunded, the original owner's QR pass (`qrToken`) is immediately revoked across all live gate scanners (`Security Validation Failed: Revoked Token`).

### 3. 👑 The Organizer Command Center
A high-velocity dashboard built for Society Presidents, Vice Presidents, and Event Directors (`society-dashboard`).
- **Hero Analytics Suite:** 8 real-time metric cards tracking *Gross Revenue, Net Take-Home (`Gross - Fee - Refunds`), Tickets Sold, Available Inventory, Pending Verifications, Booked Passes, Cancelled Orders,* and *Refund Requests*.
- **Interactive Visualizations:** `Recharts` powered time-series revenue graphs and ticket status pie breakdowns.
- **Double-Capacity Protection:** Strict venue safety locks preventing overcrowding and blocking capacity reductions below already sold ticket counts.
- **Automated Waitlists:** Automated background workers instantly reallocate sold-out slots to queued students whenever an attendee cancels or a payment receipt is rejected.

### 4. 🔒 Role-Based Access Control & Financial Transparency
- **Multi-Tiered RBAC:** Delegate operations without exposing sensitive credentials. Invite officers as *Event Managers* (can edit events and verify payments) or volunteers as *Gate Scanners* (can only access the `/scan` mobile app).
- **KYC & Faculty Advisor Audits:** Protect students by ensuring every society submits president `CNIC` verification and supervising Faculty Advisor endorsement (`☑️ Verified Society`).
- **Clean Wire Withdrawals:** Request direct bank electronic wire payouts (`IBFT`) of your clean Net Revenue directly to your registered society ledger.
