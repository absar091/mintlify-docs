    # UniTix — University Event Ticketing, Gate Operations & Financial Settlement Platform

    **UniTix** is an enterprise-grade platform that digitizes every step of university campus event management: from the moment a society registers itself and publishes an event, through student ticket purchases and payment verification, to real-time gate check-in operations (including zero-internet underground venues), automated financial settlements, and post-event certificate issuance.

    It is live at `https://unitix.aarx.online` and serves multiple universities, societies, and thousands of students.

    ---

    ## Why UniTix Was Built — The Real Problems on Pakistani University Campuses

    University campus events in Pakistan (and across South Asia) have historically been managed using fragmented, manual, and insecure methods. The consequences are serious:

    ### Problem 1: Ticket Counterfeiting and Gate-Crashing

    Societies print paper tokens or send static QR code images via WhatsApp. Students forward these images to classmates. At the gate, volunteers have no way to verify whether a screenshot is legitimate or duplicated. Result: hundreds of unauthorized entries, safety violations, and revenue theft.

    **How UniTix Solves This**: Every purchased ticket generates a dynamic, time-rotating security pass. The pass visuals refresh on the student's screen every few seconds. A saved screenshot becomes stale and is rejected when scanned at the gate. The original ticket holder must present their live screen.

    ### Problem 2: No-Internet Venue Failures

    University auditoriums, basements, and sports complexes often have weak or zero cellular reception. Cloud-dependent ticketing systems simply stop working. Gate staff resort to paper lists or let everyone in.

    **How UniTix Solves This**: The Gate Scanner APK provides two offline modes:

    - **Offline Manifest Mode**: Before entering the venue, gate staff download the complete verified attendee manifest to their phone's local storage. Every scan is validated locally without any internet connection.
    - **Event Hub P2P Mesh Mode**: Multiple gate phones connect directly to each other via local wireless mesh networking, sharing scan records in real time to prevent duplicate cross-gate entries — all without cellular data.

    ### Problem 3: Dangerous Overcrowding and Fire Safety Hazards

    Societies sell more tickets than their venue can physically hold. Multiple ticket tiers (Early Bird, VIP, General) are managed in separate spreadsheets with no aggregate tracking. Result: fire safety violations and dangerously packed halls.

    **How UniTix Solves This**: The Event Builder enforces a **Double-Capacity Hard Stop**. Organizers set an overall venue safety capacity (e.g., 500 people). The system aggregates ticket sales across ALL tiers in real time. The instant total registrations reach 500, all ticket sales halt automatically. Capacity cannot be reduced below already-sold ticket counts.

    ### Problem 4: Financial Chaos, Missing Cash, and Disputes

    Societies collect fees through personal bank accounts, JazzCash, or cash-in-hand. There is no structured ledger. After the event, discrepancies arise between collected money and expected revenue. Refund disputes are handled informally over WhatsApp.

    **How UniTix Solves This**: UniTix provides a structured dual payment rail:

    - **Automated Card Processing**: Instant digital checkout.
    - **Manual Bank Transfer Verification**: Students upload payment receipt screenshots. Society executives verify each receipt inside the platform with full audit trail.
    - **Society Wallet**: Real-time dashboard showing Gross Revenue, dynamic Platform Fee deductions, Escrow Holds for upcoming events, Available Balance, and direct bank wire payout requests.
    - **Dynamic 3-Tier Fee System**: The platform fee is not a fixed 2.5% for everyone. It follows a 3-level priority hierarchy: (1) An event-specific locked fee rate set at approval time takes highest priority. (2) If no event-level fee exists, a society-specific custom fee rate (negotiated with the platform) is used. (3) If neither exists, the system-wide global default of 2.5% applies. This means established, high-volume societies can negotiate reduced rates, and the platform can set special rates for specific events. Once an event is approved, the fee percentage is permanently locked to that event and never changes, even if the global or society rate changes later.

    ### Problem 5: No Post-Event Accountability

    After events conclude, there is no structured way to verify who actually attended. Certificates are distributed arbitrarily or not at all. Employers and graduate schools have no mechanism to verify claims of event participation.

    **How UniTix Solves This**: Gate scan records automatically mark attendees as physically present. Students whose passes were scanned at the gate can claim verified digital attendance certificates. Each certificate carries a unique verification ID and public QR code that employers or university admissions offices can independently verify at any time.

    ## How the 3-Tier Dynamic Platform Fee Works

    The UniTix platform fee is **not** a fixed 2.5% for every society and every event. It uses a hierarchical resolution system with three priority levels:

    | Priority | Fee Source | When It Applies | Example |
    | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
    | **1 (Highest)** | Event-Locked Fee | When administrators approve an event, they can set a specific fee percentage for that individual event. Once locked, this rate is permanently attached to the event and never changes. | A charity gala is approved with a 0% fee as a platform sponsorship. |
    | **2** | Society Custom Fee | If no event-level fee is set, the platform checks whether the society has a negotiated custom fee rate. Large, high-volume societies may have arrangements for reduced rates. | A major university society negotiates a 1.5% custom rate across all their events. |
    | **3 (Default)** | Global Platform Default | If neither an event-level nor a society-level fee exists, the system-wide global default applies. The current global default is 2.5%. | A new society with no special arrangement pays the standard 2.5% rate. |

    **Important Fee Rules**:

    - **Fee is calculated on the original ticket price**: If a society offers a promo code discount (e.g., 50% off), the platform fee is still calculated on the full original base price, not the discounted amount the student pays. This means societies absorb the cost of their own promotional discounts.
    - **Fee is permanently locked at event approval**: The moment an event is approved and published, the resolved effective fee percentage is permanently stamped onto that event record. Even if the global default or the society's custom rate changes later, previously approved events retain their originally locked rate.
    - **Visible in the Wallet**: The effective fee percentage for each event is displayed in the Society Wallet financial breakdown, so organizers always know exactly what rate applies to each of their events.
