## What makes UniTix different?

UniTix isn't just a generic event calendar—it is a complete ecosystem built for the unique needs of university life. 

### For Students
- **Never miss out:** Browse all events happening across your campus in one place.
- **Fair access:** Automated waitlists ensure that if an event sells out, you still have a fair chance to get a ticket if someone cancels.
- **Digital passes:** Your phone is your ticket. Fast, reliable entry at the gate.

### For Organizers
- **Complete control:** Manage capacities, create ticket tiers (e.g., Early Bird, VIP), and showcase your sponsors.
- **Secure check-in:** Forgery-proof digital tickets and a dedicated scanner app mean the end of paper lists and gate crashing.
- **Financial clarity:** Real-time revenue tracking and seamless payout requests directly to your society's bank account.
