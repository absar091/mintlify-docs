    # Your Campus. Your Events. Completely Reengineered.

    **UniTix** is the enterprise-grade event management, fraud-proof digital ticketing, gate operations, and automated financial settlement platform purpose-built for universities, student bodies, and campus societies.

    ---

    ## 🎯 Why UniTix Exists (The Problem with Legacy Campus Ticketing)

    Historically, campus event organizing has suffered from fragmented, insecure, and manual workflows:
    - **Google Forms & WhatsApp Spreadsheets**: Lost records, manual payment receipt reconciliation errors, and human bottlenecks.
    - **Counterfeit Paper Passes & Screenshot Abuse**: Attendees forward static QR codes or screenshot barcodes to multiple classmates, causing widespread gate-crashing.
    - **Dangerous Overcrowding**: Inability to enforce strict physical venue capacity limits, leading to fire safety hazards.
    - **Disorganized Financials & Cash Bags**: Delayed society funds, lack of automated fee transparency, and disputes over ticket cancellations.
    - **Zero Offline Infrastructure**: Entrance gates collapse when underground auditoriums or crowded halls experience mobile network outages.

    **UniTix replaces this broken system with a unified, cloud-native campus operating ecosystem.**

    ---

    ## 🏛️ What UniTix Solves — Feature Architecture

    | Challenge | Legacy Method | The UniTix Solution |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | **Ticket Counterfeiting** | Static paper or forwarded screenshots | **Dynamic Rotating Security Passes**: Pass refreshes automatically every few seconds on screen; screenshots expire and are rejected at the gate. |
    | **No-Internet Venues** | Paper checklist or unverified entry | **Offline Manifest Engine & P2P Mesh**: Download verified attendee manifests on-device and pair gate phones wirelessly without cellular data. |
    | **Overcrowding & Safety** | Guesswork across multiple tiers | **Double-Capacity Hard Stop**: Sales automatically halt across all tiers the instant the venue's overall safety capacity is reached. |
    | **Payment Reconciliation** | Manually checking banking SMS alerts | **Dual Payment Rails**: Automated card processing + structured manual bank transfer/EasyPaisa slip verification with transaction logs. |
    | **Post-Event Validation** | Manual paper certificates | **Automated Verifiable Credentials**: Gate scan unlocks official attendance certificates with public verification QR codes. |

    ---

    ## 🚀 Complete Platform Capabilities by User Role

    ### 🎓 For Students (The Attendee Experience)
    - **Campus Marketplace**: Discover workshops, summits, competitions, and concerts with smart university and society filters.
    - **Dynamic Rotating Passes**: Display high-contrast digital passes with live attendance status indicators.
    - **One-Click Ticket Transfers**: Safely transfer a ticket to a classmate; the old pass is instantly revoked while a fresh pass is issued.
    - **Automated Waitlists**: Join queues for sold-out events and receive automatic reallocation notices when spots open.
    - **Official Invoices & Spending Ledger**: Track all transactions and download PDF payment receipts.
    - **Dispute Operations**: Submit ticket appeals and receive transparent platform arbitration.

    ### 🏰 For Society Presidents & Organizers
    - **8-Step Event Builder**: Configure multi-tier pricing, hard capacity stops, Spotify ambient audio, and FAQ accordions.
    - **5-Tab Marketing Hub**: Launch percentage or fixed promo codes, send attendee email broadcasts, auto-generate digital flyers, and track referral links.
    - **Sponsorships Hub**: Manage sponsor tiers and automate brand logo placement on PDF tickets and public event pages.
    - **Live Gate Telemetry Monitor**: Real-time velocity gauge (scans/min), live check-in streams, and queue density tracking.
    - **Society Financial Wallet**: Monitor gross revenue, platform fee deductions (2.5%), escrow holds, and request direct bank wire payouts.
    - **Team Roles & Permissions**: Delegate operations to Event Managers, Payment Verifiers, and Gate Volunteers.

    ### 📱 For Gate Security & Volunteer Scanners
    - **Native Android APK & Web App**: High-speed camera verification with sub-second response times.
    - **4-Metric Live Bar**: Real-time header displaying *Tickets Sold, Checked In, Remaining, and Max Capacity*.
    - **Three Validation Modes**: Online Cloud, Offline Local Database, and Multi-Gate Peer Pairing.
    - **Instant Visual & Audio Alerts**: Green (Valid entry), Red (Already scanned / duplicate), Amber (Wrong gate/event), and Grey (Expired screenshot).
