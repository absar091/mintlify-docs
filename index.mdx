    # Your Campus. Your Events. Completely Reengineered.

    Welcome to the official documentation for **UniTix** (`https://unitix.aarx.online`), the all-in-one digital ticketing, real-time gate telemetry, and campus event management platform.

    UniTix replaces chaotic manual spreadsheets, paper tokens, and unverified forms with a modern, high-speed ecosystem tailored for student attendees, society presidents, and venue gate teams.

    ---

    ## 🏛️ The Four Core Pillars of UniTix

    ### 1. The Student Discovery & Checkout Experience
    A responsive campus marketplace where students discover workshops, galas, hackathons, and sports tournaments.
    - **Smart Campus Filters**: Filter by university, verified society, category, or event date.
    - **Dynamic Ticket Passes**: Fraud-proof, time-rotating dynamic QR passes that eliminate static screenshot counterfeiting.
    - **Flexible Payment Options**: Instant digital checkout or manual bank transfer / mobile wallet receipt uploads with automated status tracking.

    ### 2. The 8-Step Event Builder & Multimedia Engine
    Comprehensive tools for society organizers to design high-converting event pages in minutes.
    - **Multi-Tier Ticketing**: Configure Early Bird, VIP, and General passes with individual quotas and per-student limits.
    - **Double-Capacity Safety Locks**: Automatic hard stops prevent physical venue overcrowding and fire safety violations.
    - **Interactive Multimedia**: Embed Spotify ambient soundtracks and custom FAQ accordions directly on public event pages.

    ### 3. High-Velocity Gate Operations & Offline Verification
    The native **Gate Scanner Mobile APK** and **Live Gate Telemetry Monitor** empower entrance staff to admit thousands of attendees rapidly.
    - **4 Live Attendance Metrics**: Real-time tracking of *Sold, Checked In, Remaining,* and *Venue Capacity*.
    - **Zero-Internet Manifest Mode**: Download verified attendee lists before entering underground halls to scan 100% offline.
    - **Event Hub P2P Mesh Pairing**: Multiple gate phones connect directly via local wireless mesh to eliminate duplicate cross-gate entries without cellular data.

    ### 4. Transparent Financials & Dispute Arbitration
    - **Society Wallet**: Transparent breakdown of Gross Revenue, Platform Fees (2.5%), Escrow Holds, and Available Balance.
    - **Automated Bank Payouts**: Secure wire disbursements directly to verified society bank accounts.
    - **Dispute Operations Center**: Independent platform arbitration ensuring fair treatment for ticket appeals and cancellations.
