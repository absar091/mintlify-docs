# Cancellation Policy

UniTix provides a transparent, self-service ticket cancellation process for university event attendees and campus societies.

---

## 1. Organizer Policy Options: Refundable vs. Non-Refundable

Every university society and organizer specifies the cancellation and refund terms applicable to their event:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Refund-Enabled Events" icon="check">
    If the event offers the **Tiered Refund Policy**, cancelling your registration automatically initiates a payment refund according to the published time schedule (100%, 80%, or 50%).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Non-Refundable Events" icon="ban">
    If an event is designated as non-refundable, you can still **cancel your pass to release your seat reservation** for other attendees, or **transfer your ticket for free to a fellow student**. The registration fee remains non-refundable.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## 2. Tiered Refund Schedule (For Refund-Enabled Events)

When an event organizer permits refunds under the standard tiered schedule, refund amounts are calculated as follows:

| Cancellation Time Window | Student Refund (%) | Retained Processing Fee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Within 24 hours of purchase** *(and at least 48h before event)* | **100% Full Refund** | **0%** (Zero fee) |
| **7 days or more before event** | **80% Partial Refund** | **20%** Processing Fee |
| **48 hours to 7 days before event** | **50% Partial Refund** | **50%** Processing Fee |
| **Under 48 hours before event** | **0% (No Refund)** | **100%** (Locked Capacity) |

---

## 3. Self-Service Attendee Cancellation

Attendees can cancel their registrations directly through the student portal prior to the event:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Tickets">
    Log into your dashboard and navigate to [My Tickets](https://unitix.aarx.online/dashboard/tickets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Ticket">
    Choose the specific event pass you wish to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Cancellation">
    Click **Request Cancellation / Refund**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Refund Calculation">
    Review the refund calculation or non-refundable cancellation summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Cancellation">
    Click **Confirm Cancellation** to release the ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 4. Free Ticket Transfer Alternative

If your ticket is non-refundable or within the final 48-hour window, you can transfer your ticket to any fellow student **100% free of charge**:
- Simply input their registered university email address or student roll number.
- The recipient receives a fresh, dynamic QR pass immediately.
- The original pass is securely invalidated.

---

## 5. Event Cancellation by Organizer (100% Guarantee)

If an event is cancelled entirely by the hosting society or university administration:
- **100% Full Refund** is issued automatically to all registered ticket holders.
- **Zero deduction** is applied.
- Funds are reversed to the original payment channel within 3 to 10 business days.
