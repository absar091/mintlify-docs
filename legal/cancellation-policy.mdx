# Cancellation Policy

UniTix provides an automated and transparent ticket cancellation framework designed for event attendees and university societies.

---

## 1. Society Autonomy: Refund-Enabled vs. No-Refund Events

Every university society decides whether to permit refunds on an event-by-event basis:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="When Society Allows Refunds" icon="check">
    If the hosting society enables the **Tiered Refund Policy**, cancelling your ticket automatically triggers an electronic refund calculated according to the timing schedule (100%, 80%, or 50%).
  </Card>
  <Card title="When Society Selects 'No Refunds'" icon="ban">
    If marked non-refundable, you can still **cancel your ticket to release your seat slot** for others or **transfer your ticket for free to a friend**. However, **no monetary refund is issued (0%)**, and the society retains 100% of the funds.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## 2. Cancellation Mechanics for No-Refund Events
When a ticket is cancelled under a non-refundable policy:
- **Slot Restored:** The event capacity slot is immediately restored and made available for purchase by other students (unless ticket sales are closed).
- **Society Retains 100% Funds:** No deduction is made from the society's wallet or dashboard. The society retains full ticket revenue as cancellation penalty earnings.
- **No Payout Queued to Super Admin:** The platform does not generate an administrative refund payout request.

---

## 3. Tiered Cancellation & Refund Schedule (For Refund-Enabled Events)

When an organizer selects the recommended **Tiered Refund Policy**, the following schedule applies:

| Cancellation Time Window | Student Refund (%) | Retained Fee Split |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Within 24 hours of purchase** *(and at least 48h before event)* | **100% Full Refund** | **0%** (Zero penalty) |
| **7 days or more before event** | **80% Partial Refund** | **20%** (10% Society, 10% Platform) |
| **48 hours to 7 days before event** | **50% Partial Refund** | **50%** (30% Society, 20% Platform) |
| **Under 48 hours before event** | **0% (No Refund)** | **100%** (Locked Seat Capacity) |

<Note>
  Retained processing fees cover merchant gateway interchange, banking clearing, automated ticket revocation, and administrative infrastructure.
</Note>

---

## 4. Self-Service Attendee Cancellation

Attendees can cancel their tickets directly through the student portal:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to My Tickets">
    Open your student dashboard and select [My Tickets](https://unitix.aarx.online/dashboard/tickets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Ticket">
    Click on the specific event ticket pass you want to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Cancellation / Refund">
    Click **Request Cancellation / Refund**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Refund Schedule">
    The system displays your exact refund amount and cancellation fee based on the event's policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm & Process">
    Click **Confirm Cancellation**. The ticket is released and payment reversal is initiated automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 5. Free Ticket Transfer Alternative

If your ticket is within the non-refundable window (under 48 hours before the event) or the event is marked "No Refunds", you can transfer your ticket to any fellow student **100% free of charge**:
- Simply input their registered university email or student roll number.
- The recipient receives a fresh, dynamic QR pass immediately.
- The original pass is securely invalidated.

---

## 6. Organizer Cancellation Guarantee (100% Full Refund)

If an event is cancelled by the organizing society, campus club, or university administration:
- **100% Full Refund** is issued automatically to all registered ticket holders.
- **Zero deduction** is applied.
- Funds are reversed to the original payment method within 3 to 10 business days.
