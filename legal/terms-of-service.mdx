These General Terms of Service govern your use of the UniTix platform. We provide an advanced ticketing infrastructure for University Societies to securely manage event access, sales, and analytics.

# Description of Service
UniTix acts strictly as an infrastructure provider. We facilitate the creation, distribution, and validation of cryptographic digital tickets between Event Organizers (Societies) and Buyers (Students). We do not organize the events ourselves.

# Ticket Sales & The Automated Waitlist
Event organizers define their own ticket capacities. If an event sells out, buyers may join an Automated Waitlist. If a ticket becomes available (via cancellation or capacity expansion), the waitlist mechanism will reserve the ticket for the next person in line for exactly 24 hours. Failure to purchase within this window results in the ticket passing to the next user.

# Cancellation, Refunds, & Penalty Tiers
Because UniTix acts as an intermediary, **all refunds are strictly governed by the organizing Society's configuration for that specific event.**

- **The Dynamic Refund Policy:** Organizers may configure specific penalty tiers based on time. For example, an organizer may set a policy where cancellations 7 days prior yield an 80% refund to the student, while cancellations within 48 hours yield a 0% refund.
- **Platform Fees are Non-Refundable:** Regardless of the society's refund policy, the UniTix processing fee (standard 2.5%) is never refunded.
- **Event Cancellation:** If an organizer cancels an event entirely, the organizer is legally responsible for refunding all tickets. UniTix will assist the society in mass-crediting student accounts, but the funds are sourced from the society.

# Ticket Transfers & Security
Digital tickets generated by UniTix are secured via cryptographic JWTs (JSON Web Tokens). 

- **Screenshots are Invalid:** The Gate Scanner app tracks live validation. Sending a screenshot of a QR code to another person will result in a failed scan at the gate.
- **Official Transfer Mechanism:** Tickets may only be transferred using the official `Transfer Ticket` button within the dashboard, which invalidates the original ticket and generates a new secure QR code for the recipient. Transferring tickets outside this system is strictly prohibited.
