title: "Privacy Policy"
description: "How UniTix collects, uses, and protects your data."
---

At UniTix ("we," "our," or "us"), securing your personal and financial data is our highest priority. Because we process financial transactions and issue cryptographically secure digital tickets, we are legally required to collect specific identifying information to prevent fraud and maintain the integrity of our platform.

# Information We Collect from Students
When you register as a student or purchase a ticket, we collect the following to ensure tickets are issued to real, verifiable students:
- **Identity Data:** Full Legal Name, University Roll/Registration Number, and Profile Image.
- **Contact Data:** Primary Email Address.
- **Financial Data:** When purchasing tickets, we collect uploaded screenshots of payment receipts (e.g., JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer) or securely process credit card details via our PCI-compliant payment gateways.
- **Usage & Device Data:** IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and platform interactions.

# Information We Collect from Society Organizers (KYC)
To protect students from fraudulent events and to comply with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations regarding payouts, we enforce a strict **Know Your Customer (KYC)** protocol for all Society Organizers. During society registration, we mandate the collection of:
- **Government Identification:** The front and back images of the Society President/Admin's CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card).
- **University Documentation:** Official University Letters verifying the Society's existence.
- **Financial Routing Data:** Official Bank Account details (IBAN, Account Name, Branch Code) for processing ticket payouts.
- **Faculty Advisor Details:** The name, email, and contact number of the supervising University Faculty member responsible for the society.

# How We Use Your Information
We do not collect this data arbitrarily. Your information is strictly utilized to:
- **Verify Authenticity:** We use CNICs, University IDs, and Faculty Advisor contacts to manually verify that every Society on UniTix is authentic, preventing scam events from being hosted on our platform.
- **Process Payouts securely:** Bank details are used exclusively by UniTix Operations to disburse event earnings to the organizers.
- **Enforce Ticket Security:** Student names and Profile Images are directly tied to the digital QR tickets. When scanned at the gate, this data is displayed to the scanner to prevent ticket scalping and transfer fraud.

# Information Sharing and Disclosure
We absolutely **do not sell** your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared under the following circumstances:
- **With Event Organizers:** When a student buys a ticket, the specific Society organizing that event gains access to the student's Name and University ID to facilitate check-in and manage attendance.
- **With Law Enforcement:** If required by a court order, subpoena, or to prevent imminent physical harm or financial fraud, we will share identity documents (like CNICs) with appropriate legal authorities.
- **With Service Providers:** Encrypted data is shared with our secure cloud hosting providers (Google Cloud/Firebase) and payment processors strictly for operational purposes.

# Data Security & Storage
All sensitive documents, including uploaded payment receipts and CNIC images, are uploaded directly to secured Google Cloud Storage buckets. Access to these buckets is restricted via strict IAM (Identity and Access Management) rules and Firebase Security Rules. Only authenticated UniTix Platform Management personnel and the uploading user can access these files.
