# Privacy & Data Security Policy

At **UniTix** (`https://unitix.aarx.online`), safeguarding student privacy, personal identity, and organizer financial records is our highest priority. We follow strict **Privacy-by-Design** principles that respect student dignity, cultural values, and international data protection standards (`GDPR / PDPA`).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. From Student Attendees
To verify academic status and issue forgery-proof digital passes:
- **Core Identity:** Full Legal Name, Primary Email Address, and Phone Number.
- **Academic Verification:** University Name, Academic Department, and Official University Roll/Registration Number (e.g., `2022-CS-101`).
- **Transaction Proofs:** Screenshots of manual bank transfers (`JazzCash`, `EasyPaisa`, `IBFT`) and transaction reference IDs (`TxID`) submitted during manual checkouts for organizer verification.

### B. Cultural & Personal Privacy Protection
- **No Facial Photo Display on Scanner Screens:** Volunteer gate scanners never receive or display student photographs on their screens. This ensures full privacy and dignity for all students, including female students who observe hijab or niqab.
- **Zero-Trust Token Verification:** Ticket validation is performed using 15-second dynamic time-rotating cryptographic tokens and official university roll numbers, eliminating any need for facial recognition or photo scanning.

### C. From Society Organizers & Presidents
To comply with financial accountability and verification standards:
- **Official Identifiers:** Society Name, Campus Handle, and Faculty Advisor credentials (`Name, Title, Official Email`).
- **President KYC Documents:** National ID Card (`CNIC`) Front and Back photographs submitted during society registration.
- **Banking Credentials:** Official Bank Account Title, Bank Name, and International Bank Account Number (`IBAN`) for ticket sales payout transfers.

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Used & Processed
We process personal information strictly for core platform functionality:
1. **Cryptographic Ticket Issuance:** Generating dynamic, time-rotating security passes tied to your roll number and ticket ID.
2. **Gate Check-in & Security:** Displaying verified attendee Name, Roll Number, Department, and Ticket Tier on gate scanners for physical student card matching.
3. **Financial Reconciliation:** Verifying payment receipts against society accounts and executing direct wire payouts (`IBFT`).
4. **Automated Waitlists & Notifications:** Dispatching real-time notifications when tickets are confirmed, transferred, or reallocated.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure Boundaries
**UniTix never sells, rents, or monetizes personal data to third-party advertisers.**
- **Society Access:** When you reserve a ticket, the organizing society gains access to your Name, Roll Number, Email, Department, and Check-in Status for attendee management and `.CSV` manifest generation.
- **Volunteer Scanner Isolation:** Volunteer gate staff can only view your Name, Roll Number, and Ticket Tier during the split-second scan. They cannot download attendee manifests, export personal emails, or view financial ledgers.
- **Legal Compliance:** We reserve the right to disclose records to university administration or law enforcement if required by lawful authority in cases of fraud or campus safety threats.

---

## 4. Encryption & Data Storage
- **In-Transit Encryption:** All communication is encrypted using industry-standard `TLS 1.3 / HTTPS`.
- **At-Rest Storage:** User records, event data, and cryptographic tickets are stored securely inside encrypted cloud databases protected by strict Identity & Access Management (`IAM`) security boundaries.
- **Financial & KYC Storage:** Sensitive CNIC identity images and bank receipts are stored inside isolated, encrypted cloud object storage governed by short-lived, signed access tokens.

---

## 5. Your Data Rights & Deletion Requests
As a registered student or organizer:
- **Access & Corrections:** You can update your profile details and password anytime inside `/dashboard/profile`.
- **Account Deletion:** You may request permanent deletion of your account and personal identifiers by contacting `support@unitix.com`. Financial audit records and completed payout logs are retained as required by financial regulations.
