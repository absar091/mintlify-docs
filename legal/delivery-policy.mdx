# Digital Product Delivery & Fulfillment Policy

UniTix operates a high-speed digital ticketing infrastructure. This policy outlines how digital passes and electronic tickets are issued, accessed, and verified across events hosted on **UniTix** (`https://unitix.aarx.online`).

---

## 1. 100% Digital Intangible Goods (Zero Shipping Delay)
UniTix strictly sells **non-physical, digitally delivered event access passes and attendee registrations**. 
- There is **no physical shipping**, no postal transit time, and no courier dispatch fee.
- All digital passes are fulfilled instantaneously over secure encrypted cloud channels.

---

## 2. Instant Electronic Delivery Channels
Upon successful transaction completion via our integrated payment gateway (Cards, Mobile Wallets, 1Link Bank Transfers, or SBP Raast QR), your access pass is delivered within **under 2 seconds** across multiple platforms:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="In-Account Dashboard" icon="user">
    Available immediately under **"My Tickets"** in the student dashboard (`/dashboard/tickets`). Features a high-security, dynamic time-rotating QR pass.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automated Email Pass" icon="envelope">
    An automated transaction receipt and permanent secure pass link are dispatched to the buyer's registered email address.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Offline Student App" icon="mobile">
    Attendees can present their dynamic rotating passes in basements or zero-connectivity auditoriums via the **UniTix Student APK**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## 3. Dynamic QR Code Security & Gatekeeper Validation
To eliminate counterfeiting and screenshot reselling:
- Every pass embeds a cryptographically signed JSON Web Token (JWT) with time-based rotating validation.
- When presented at the venue gate, the authorized **UniTix Gate Scanner App** verifies the pass in under one second.
- The entrance scan is logged with an immutable timestamp, serving as definitive **Proof of Fulfillment and Attendance**.

---

If you have completed your payment but do not see your pass under "My Tickets" within 60 seconds:
1. Refresh your dashboard at [https://unitix.aarx.online/dashboard/tickets](https://unitix.aarx.online/dashboard/tickets).
2. Check your registered email's spam folder for confirmation from `support@aarx.online`.
