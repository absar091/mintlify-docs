# Official Refund, Cancellation & Dispute Policy

UniTix processes payments on behalf of verified University Societies and campus event organizers. This policy outlines the conditions under which refunds are issued, how they are calculated, and the timeline for receiving funds.

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
UniTix provides digital ticketing and payment infrastructure for university student societies and campus events across Pakistan. This policy governs how ticket refunds, cancellations, and disputes are handled across all events hosted on UniTix (`https://unitix.aarx.online`).

---

## 2. Organizer Refund Policies & UniTix Recommendation
Each University Society and event organizer has the full autonomy to set their own refund policy on an event-by-event basis (e.g., an organizer may enable refunds on an academic conference, but designate No Refunds on a fixed-catering dinner or tournament). The specific policy applicable to an event is clearly displayed during checkout.

<Tip>
  **Platform Recommendation:** UniTix natively automates refund dispatches and **strongly recommends that organizers enable the Tiered Refund Policy**. Transparent cancellation terms build student buyer confidence, remove ticket purchase hesitation, and consistently lead to higher overall ticket sales.
</Tip>

---

## 3. Event Cancellation Guarantee
If an event is cancelled entirely by the Event Organizer or University Administration:
- Attendees are entitled to a **100% full refund of the ticket face value**.
- Refunds are initiated automatically back to the original payment source.

---

## 4. Event Postponement and Changes
- If an event is postponed, your ticket remains valid for the rescheduled date unless the Organizer announces otherwise. Any refunds for postponed events are subject to the Organizer's refund policy and applicable law.
- Minor changes to the event (including venue alterations within the campus, schedule adjustments, or speaker/performer replacements) do not automatically qualify for a refund unless determined by the Organizer.

---

## 5. Student Cancellation (Tiered Refund Schedule)
Where an Organizer has selected the **Tiered Refund Policy**, refunds are calculated according to the following schedule:

| Cancellation Time Window | Student Refund (%) | Retained Fee Split |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Within 24 hours of purchase** *(and at least 48h before event)* | **100%** | **0%** (Full refund) |
| **7 days or more before event** | **80%** | **20%** *(10% Society, 10% Platform)* |
| **48 hours to 7 days before event** | **50%** | **50%** *(30% Society, 20% Platform)* |
| **Under 48 hours before event** | **0%** *(No eligibility)* | **100%** (Slot cannot be reallocated) |

<Warning>
  **Last-Minute Purchases (<48 Hours Before Event Start):**  
  If you purchase a ticket within 48 hours of the event taking place, **the 48-Hour Operational Lock strictly overrides the 24-hour grace period**. Because event organizers have already finalized security manifests, catering orders, and badge printing, cancellations and refunds are completely closed under 48 hours.  
  
  **Can't attend?** You can still transfer your ticket **100% free of charge** to a fellow classmate or friend using the in-app **Transfer Pass** feature.
</Warning>

*The retained cancellation amount covers payment gateway interchange charges, platform operating costs, fraud prevention, and administrative processing.*

---

## 6. Refund Timeline & Processing
- **Self-Service Portal:** Eligible refunds can be requested directly through the **My Tickets** section of the UniTix Student Dashboard.
- **Settlement Timeline:** Depending on your bank or mobile wallet provider (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Debit/Credit Cards), funds generally reflect within several business days.
- **Exceptions for "No Refunds" Events:** If an event is designated as "No Refunds" or outside the policy window, UniTix cannot override the organizer's terms without explicit authorization from the hosting society.

---

## 7. Transparent Calculation Example (Rs 2,000 Ticket)
To ensure complete transparency, here is the exact financial breakdown for a **Rs 2,000 ticket**:

- **Ticket Price:** Rs 2,000
- **Online Gateway Fee:** Rs 61
- **Total Paid at Checkout:** Rs 2,061

### Tier 1 Refund (Cancelled 8 days prior to event):
- **Student Refund (80%):** **Rs 1,600 returned to student**
- **Society Retains (10%):** Rs 200
- **UniTix Retains (10%):** Rs 200
- **Gateway Fee:** Non-refundable (third-party payment processor fee)

---

## 8. Non-refundable Charges
Unless otherwise stated during checkout, platform service fees and third-party payment processing fees are non-refundable.

---

## 9. Ticket Transfers (Alternative to Cancellation)
Instead of cancelling, attendees may transfer their ticket pass to another verified student through the UniTix dashboard:
- Transfers must be executed via the official in-app Transfer Ticket feature.
- Upon completion, the previous QR code is cryptographically revoked and a fresh token is generated for the new attendee.
- Unauthorized sales, screenshots, or private scalping are strictly prohibited.

---

## 10. Fraud & Abuse Protection
UniTix reserves the right to deny refunds where fraudulent activity, payment tampering, or abuse of the refund system is detected. Initiating unauthorized bank chargebacks after receiving a valid digital ticket may result in temporary account suspension pending investigation.
