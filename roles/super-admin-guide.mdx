# Super Administration

The **Super Admin** role is reserved for the owners and creators of the UniTix platform. Super Admins have global access to all data across all universities and societies.

## 1. Society Approvals

When a new student organization wants to use UniTix, they fill out the `/register-society` form.

To prevent spam and fraudulent organizations from creating events on campus, their account is placed into a "Pending Review" state.

The Super Admin must log into the Admin Dashboard, review the society's credentials and faculty advisor details, and explicitly click **Approve**. Once approved, the society's Firebase token is granted the `society_admin` custom claim.

## 2. Payout Processing

UniTix acts as the escrow for all ticket sales. When a society requests to withdraw their funds to pay for their event catering or venue, the request goes to the Super Admin.

1. Navigate to the **Global Payouts** dashboard.
2. Review the Society's requested amount against their current total wallet balance.
3. Wire the funds to the Society's provided bank account via your real-world banking app.
4. Click **Mark as Completed** on the dashboard. This deducts the withdrawn amount from the society's virtual wallet.
