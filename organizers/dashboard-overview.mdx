# Your Command Center

Once your society registration is approved by UniTix Management, you will gain access to the **Society Dashboard**. This is your central hub for managing events, auditing finances, and controlling your team.

## Navigation 

The left sidebar contains all your tools:
- **Events:** Create new events, view active capacities, and manage your calendar.
- **Analytics:** High-level overview of ticket sales, page views, and revenue.
- **Wallet & Payouts:** Track your earnings and request bank transfers.
- **Sponsors:** Add corporate sponsor logos to your event pages.
- **Members & Scanners:** Invite event managers and gate scanners to your team.

## Customizing Your Sidebar (Pinning)

We know that every society operates differently. Some admins spend all their time in Analytics, while others focus purely on Event Creation.

To make your workflow faster, UniTix allows you to **Pin** your most-used features to the top of your sidebar.

1. Hover over any menu item in the sidebar.
2. Click the small **Pin Icon** (`📌`) that appears on the right side of the item.
3. The item will instantly jump to a new "Pinned" section at the very top of your sidebar.
4. To unpin, simply click the icon again.

*Note: Your pinned preferences are saved locally to your browser for lightning-fast loading.*
