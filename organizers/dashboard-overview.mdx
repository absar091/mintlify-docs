# Your Command Center

Once your society registration is approved, logging into UniTix will route you directly to the **Society Dashboard**. This is your central hub for all operations.

## Customizing Your Sidebar (Pinning)
Every society operates differently. To make your workflow faster, UniTix allows you to **Pin** your most-used features to the top of your sidebar.

**How to Pin an item:**
1. Hover your mouse over any menu item in the left sidebar (e.g., "Analytics").
2. Click the small **Pin Icon** (`📌`) that appears on the right edge of the item.
3. The item will instantly jump to a new "Pinned" section at the top of your sidebar.
4. To unpin, simply click the icon again.

*Note: Pinned preferences are saved securely in your browser's local storage, ensuring your layout loads instantly every time you visit.*
