# Your Command Center

Once your society registration is approved, logging into UniTix routes you directly to the **Society Dashboard**. Designed for speed and executive clarity, your dashboard serves as the nerve center for all event operations, ticket management, and financial analytics.

## 1. Top Navigation & Context Switcher
At the top of your screen, you'll find your society header and quick-access controls:
- **Greeting & Society Identity:** Displays your society logo, name, and current time-sensitive greeting.
- **Current Event Switcher:** By default, the dashboard aggregates data across **All Events**. Use the dropdown switcher in the top right to filter all metrics, charts, and timelines to a single specific event instantly.
- **Notification Bell & Profile:** Access system alerts, announcements, and quick settings.

---

## 2. Hero Analytics Section (The 8 Metric Cards)
Directly beneath the header lies the **Hero Analytics Suite**, giving you real-time visibility into your society's health across 8 dynamic cards:

| Metric Card | What It Tracks | How It Is Calculated |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Revenue** | Total ticket sales volume | Sum of all revenue across active events |
| **Net Revenue** | Your actual take-home earnings | `Gross Revenue - Platform Fee (2.5% or custom tier) - Refunds` |
| **Tickets Sold** | Confirmed & verified attendees | Count of tickets with status `approved` or `used` |
| **Available Tickets** | Remaining venue inventory | `Total Venue Capacity - Tickets Sold` |
| **Pending Verifications** | Manual bank transfer approvals waiting | Count of student orders requiring manual screenshot check |
| **Booked Tickets** | Reserved inventory awaiting payment | Count of tickets currently in `pending` or `pending_payment` status |
| **Cancelled Tickets** | Orders voided by students or organizers | Count of tickets marked `cancelled` |
| **Refund Requests** | Open disputes or tier adjustments | Count of active refund requests across selected events |

Each card features **interactive trend percentages** and mini **sparkline charts** (`Recharts` powered), giving you immediate visual cues on whether sales momentum is accelerating or slowing down.

---

## 3. Real-Time Revenue & Ticket Status Charts
The center of your dashboard provides deep visual analytics without leaving the main overview:
- **Revenue Over Time (Line Chart):** Tracks gross and net revenue performance across dates so you can pinpoint how marketing campaigns or early bird deadlines impacted sales surges.
- **Ticket Status Breakdown (Pie Chart):** A clean distribution chart displaying the exact ratio of `Paid`, `Booked`, `Cancelled`, `Refunded`, and `Checked In` tickets.

---

## 4. Upcoming Tasks & Activity Timeline
Never miss an operational bottleneck during crunch time:
- **Actionable Tasks:** Automatically highlights urgent action items—such as pending manual bank verifications, unassigned gate scanners, or unresolved refund disputes—with direct one-click links to resolve them.
- **Recent Activity Timeline:** A live chronological log showing real-time ticket bookings, approvals, transfers, and check-ins as they happen at the gate.

---

## 5. Customizing Your Sidebar (Pinning)
To tailor the workspace to your exact daily routine, UniTix includes an instant **Sidebar Pinning** system:
1. Hover over any navigation item in the left sidebar (e.g., *Gate Scanners*, *Analytics*, *Wallet*).
2. Click the **Pin Icon** (`📌`) on the right edge.
3. The item immediately jumps to a dedicated **Pinned** section at the top of your menu.
4. Your pinned layout is saved directly in local storage, ensuring your custom command center loads instantly every time you log in.
