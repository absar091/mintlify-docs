# The Event Builder

Creating an event on UniTix takes less than 5 minutes. Navigate to **Events** -> **Create New** to open the Event Builder.

## Basic Details
Start by providing the core information: Title, Date, Time, Venue, and an engaging Description. Upload a high-quality Banner Image to make your public page stand out.

## Setting Up Ticket Tiers

UniTix allows you to create highly customized ticket structures. You can add as many tiers as you need (e.g., Early Bird, General Admission, VIP, Non-Student).

For each tier, you must define:
1. **Name:** What is the ticket called?
2. **Price:** Is it Free, or what is the cost?
3. **Inventory Limit:** How many of *this specific ticket* are available?

If an individual tier sells out, it will automatically switch to "Waitlist Mode" while other tiers may remain open.

## Overall Venue Capacity
In addition to individual ticket limits, you must set an **Overall Event Capacity**. 
If your venue can only hold 500 people, but you have 300 Early Bird and 300 General Admission tickets, the system will automatically halt ALL sales the moment total tickets sold reaches 500, ensuring you never overcrowd the venue.

## Adding Corporate Sponsors
If your event is sponsored by brands, you can feature them prominently on your event page and digital tickets.
Navigate to the **Sponsors** tab in your dashboard, upload the sponsor's logo, and link it to your event. It will be beautifully rendered in the "Supported By" section.
