# The Analytics Dashboard

Data drives successful events. Your UniTix Analytics dashboard provides real-time insights into your ticket sales and marketing performance.

## Tracking Conversions
Navigate to the **Analytics** tab to view your core metrics:
- **Total Revenue:** The gross amount collected across all your events.
- **Tickets Sold:** The raw volume of inventory moved.
- **Page Views:** How many unique students have landed on your Event Pages.
- **Conversion Rate:** The percentage of students who viewed your page and actually completed a purchase. If your views are high but sales are low, consider adjusting your ticket prices or updating your event description!

## Exporting Attendee Lists
While the Gate Scanner app is the best way to manage entry, sometimes you need a raw spreadsheet of your attendees (e.g., for printing physical name tags).

**How to Export:**
1. Navigate to the **Events** tab.
2. Click on the specific event you want to manage.
3. In the top right corner, click **Export Attendees**.
4. The system will generate a `.csv` file containing the Names, Ticket Tiers, and check-in status of every student who reserved a ticket.
