    # Analytics & Attendee Export Reports

    The **Analytics Hub** (`/society-dashboard/analytics` and `/society-dashboard/events/[id]/analytics`) provides organizers with reporting tools to evaluate attendance trends and export verified rosters.

    ---

    ## 1. Exporting Attendee Rosters (CSV / Excel)

    ```
    Step 1: Open Event Analytics ➔ Step 2: Click Export ➔ Step 3: Download Complete Attendee CSV
    ```

    1. Go to **Society Dashboard** $\to$ **Events** $\to$ Click on your event $\to$ Click **Analytics**.
    2. Click the **Export Attendee List (CSV)** button.
    3. The file downloads with:
       - **Attendee Full Name**
       - **Registration / Roll Number**
       - **Department & University**
       - **Ticket Tier Purchased**
       - **Gate Check-In Status & Timestamp**

    ---

    ## 2. Gate Arrival Velocity & Hourly Check-In Curve

    - **Hourly Arrival Graph**: Tracks how many attendees entered the gates each hour (e.g., 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM peak arrival).
    - **No-Show Rate**: Automatically calculates the percentage of ticket holders who did not attend.
