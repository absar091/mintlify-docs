# Your Society Profile

Every Society on UniTix is granted a public-facing profile. This acts as a centralized hub where students can discover your mission, see your corporate sponsors, and view a timeline of all your upcoming and past events.

## How to Edit Your Profile
1. Navigate to the **Profile** tab in your dashboard sidebar.
2. Here, you can update your:
   - **Society Description:** A rich-text bio detailing your society's goals.
   - **Social Links:** Connect your Instagram, LinkedIn, or Facebook pages.
   - **Logo & Banner:** Upload a square logo and a 16:9 banner image.

When students click your Society Name on any event page, they are routed to this public profile. Keeping it updated is crucial for building trust and a follower base on campus.

## Managing Sponsors
If your events are backed by corporate sponsors, displaying them adds significant legitimacy to your public presence.

**How to Add Sponsors:**
1. Navigate to the **Sponsors** tab in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Sponsor**.
3. Upload their logo and enter their company name.
4. When you create an Event, you can select these sponsors from a dropdown menu. Their logos will automatically appear in the "Supported By" section on the public Event Details page.
