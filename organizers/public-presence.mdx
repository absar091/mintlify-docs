# Your Society's Public Presence

Every approved society on UniTix receives a dedicated, high-converting public landing page (`unitix.aarx.online/s/[society-slug]`). This page serves as your society's digital storefront where university students, faculty advisors, and corporate sponsors can review your mission, inspect active events, and browse past event photo galleries.

---

## 1. Customizing Your Brand Identity
To configure how your society appears across the student marketplace, navigate to **Society Profile** (`society-dashboard/profile`):
- **Official Society Name & Slug:** Set your clean URL slug (e.g., `/s/acm-chapter` or `/s/dramatics-club`).
- **Square Logo:** Upload a high-resolution 1:1 square logo (`PNG` or `WebP`). This renders on student digital QR tickets and event cards.
- **Hero Banner:** Upload a 16:9 widescreen banner image that serves as the visual header of your public profile page.
- **Mission Statement & Bio:** Write a rich-text overview of your society's vision, regular workshops, and campus activities.
- **Social Media Integration:** Connect your official Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and Discord URLs to build followers directly from ticket checkouts.

---

## 2. Earning the Verified Badge (KYC & Faculty Verification)
To protect students from fraudulent or unofficial campus clubs, UniTix awards a prominent **Verified Blue Checkmark (`☑️ Verified Society`)** alongside your society name across all event listings.

### Verification Requirements:
1. **President CNIC Audit:** The society president must upload clear front and back photos of their National ID Card during initial registration.
2. **Faculty Advisor Endorsement:** You must submit the official university email address (`@university.edu.pk`) of your supervising Professor or Faculty Advisor.
3. **Automated Verification Link:** UniTix dispatches a secure verification token directly to your Faculty Advisor via automated email. Once the advisor clicks to confirm their supervision, your society profile automatically unlocks the official **Verified Status Badge**.

---

## 3. Corporate Sponsorship Management
If your events are backed by corporate sponsors, prominently displaying their brand identities adds immense institutional credibility and helps fulfill contractual sponsor impressions.

### How to Add & Link Sponsors:
1. Navigate to the **Sponsors** tab in your dashboard sidebar and click **Add Sponsor**.
2. Upload the corporate partner's logo (`PNG` with transparent background recommended) and enter their company name and official website URL.
3. **Link to Specific Events:** When building or editing any event (`society-dashboard/events`), scroll to the **Sponsors Dropdown**.
4. Select all relevant brand partners. Their logos will automatically render:
   - In the **"Supported By"** hero section of your public Event Details page.
   - On the printable PDF and digital mobile passes issued to checked-in attendees.
