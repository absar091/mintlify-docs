# Gate Operations

The UniTix Gate Scanner is a mobile-optimized dashboard designed specifically for fast, secure, and error-free entry operations at the venue. Any user with the **Scanner**, **Event Manager**, or **Society Admin** role can access it.

Navigate to `/scan` on your mobile browser (or click **Scanner** in the sidebar) to open the app.

## How to Scan
1. Select the Event you are scanning for from the dropdown.
2. Grant browser permissions to access your device's camera.
3. Point the camera at a student's digital QR ticket.
4. The scanner processes the JWT payload and communicates with the server instantly.

## Scan Results
The scanner will display a large, color-coded alert *above* the camera viewport so you don't have to scroll down to see the result:

- **🟢 SUCCESS:** The ticket is valid and the attendee is now checked in. The student's name and ticket tier will be displayed.
- **🟡 WARNING (Already Scanned):** The ticket is authentic, but this QR code has already been scanned today. This prevents students from sending screenshots of valid tickets to friends outside the gate.
- **🔴 ERROR (Invalid/Fake):** The QR code is a forgery, belongs to a different event, or the cryptographic signature is invalid. Deny entry.

<Tip>
Ensure your scanning devices have a stable 4G/LTE connection, as the scanner requires live verification to prevent screenshot fraud.
</Tip>
