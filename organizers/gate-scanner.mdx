# Operating the Gate

The UniTix Gate Scanner is a mobile-optimized web app designed for speed. 

## How to Scan Tickets
1. On your mobile phone, open your browser and navigate to `unitix.aarx.online/scan`.
2. Log in (you must have the Society Admin, Event Manager, or Scanner role).
3. Select the Event you are currently operating from the dropdown.
4. Your browser will ask for Camera Permissions. Click **Allow**.
5. Point the camera at a student's digital QR ticket. The scanner processes the JWT payload and communicates with the server instantly.

## Reading the Results
The scanner will display a large, color-coded alert *above* the camera viewport so you don't have to scroll down.

- **🟢 SUCCESS:** The ticket is valid. The student's name and ticket tier will be displayed. Let them in!
- **🟡 WARNING (Already Scanned):** The ticket is authentic, but this QR code has already been scanned today. This prevents students from sending screenshots to friends outside the gate. Deny entry.
- **🔴 ERROR (Invalid/Fake):** The QR code is a forgery, belongs to a different event, or the cryptographic signature is invalid. Deny entry.

> [!TIP]
> Ensure your scanning devices have a stable 4G/LTE connection. The scanner requires live verification to prevent screenshot fraud.
