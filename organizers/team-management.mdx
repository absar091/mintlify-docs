# Building Your Team

As a Society Admin, you shouldn't have to do everything yourself. UniTix provides a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) system so you can safely invite your team members to help manage the platform without giving them the keys to your bank account.

Navigate to **Members** in your dashboard to send email invitations to your team.

## Available Roles

### Event Manager
Event Managers have extensive permissions to run the day-to-day operations.
- **Can:** Create events, edit ticket tiers, view the public page, and manually approve pending payment screenshots.
- **Cannot:** Access the Wallet, request payouts, change the society's bank details, or delete the society.

### Scanner
The Scanner role is highly restricted and designed exclusively for event day volunteers at the gate.
- **Can:** Log in to the Gate Scanner app, scan QR codes, and view basic attendee check-in stats.
- **Cannot:** View revenue, edit events, or access any other part of the dashboard.

## Revoking Access
If a team member leaves your society, you can instantly revoke their access from the Members tab. The next time they try to load any dashboard page, they will be securely blocked by the platform's route guards.
