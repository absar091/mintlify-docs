# Building Your Team

As a Society Admin, you have the ability to delegate operations securely using strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). 

## How to Invite a Team Member
1. Navigate to the **Members** tab in your dashboard sidebar.
2. Click **Invite Member**.
3. Enter their registered UniTix email address.
4. Select their Role (see below).
5. Click Send Invite. The member will receive an email and their dashboard will update instantly.

## Available Roles

### 1. Event Manager
Event Managers handle the heavy lifting of day-to-day operations.
- **Can:** Create events, edit ticket tiers, view the public page, and manually approve pending payment screenshots.
- **Cannot:** Access the Wallet, request payouts, change the society's bank details, or delete the society.

### 2. Scanner
The Scanner role is exclusively for event day volunteers at the gate.
- **Can:** Access the `/scan` mobile app to validate QR codes.
- **Cannot:** View revenue, edit events, or access the main dashboard.

## How to Revoke Access
If a team member leaves, you must remove them to protect your data.
1. Go to the **Members** tab.
2. Click the trash icon next to their name.
3. Their access is instantly terminated by our backend route guards.
