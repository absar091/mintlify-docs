# Financial Operations

Your **Wallet** and **Payments** tabs are the control centers for your society's revenue.

## 1. Verifying Pending Payments
If you allow students to pay via Manual Bank Transfer, JazzCash, or EasyPaisa, you must manually verify their transactions.

**How to Verify:**
1. Navigate to the **Payments** tab in your dashboard.
2. You will see a list of all "Pending" transactions, containing the Student's Name, Ticket Tier, Transaction ID, and a button to view their uploaded Screenshot.
3. **Open your Society Bank/JazzCash app** and verify that the exact amount was received from that Transaction ID.
4. If the money arrived, click **Approve** in UniTix. The student's ticket will instantly turn green (Valid).
5. If the money did not arrive or the screenshot is fake, click **Reject**.

## 2. Requesting a Payout
When your event has successfully concluded, you can withdraw your Net Earnings (Gross Revenue minus the 2.5% platform fee).

**How to Request Funds:**
1. Navigate to the **Wallet** tab.
2. Ensure your Society Bank Details (IBAN, Account Name) are securely saved in your Profile settings.
3. Check your "Available Balance".
4. Click the **Request Payout** button.

Once requested, UniTix Operations will audit the transactions and process the wire transfer directly to your society's bank account within 3-5 business days. You can track the status of your payout (Pending, Processing, Completed) directly from the Wallet.
