# Your Wallet

The **Wallet** tab in your Society Dashboard provides a real-time, transparent overview of your event revenue.

## Understanding Revenue
When an event sells tickets, the funds are securely recorded in your Wallet. The dashboard breaks down your financials into three clear metrics:
1. **Gross Revenue:** The total amount paid by students for tickets.
2. **Platform Fees:** The small percentage collected by UniTix.
3. **Net Earnings:** Your actual take-home revenue (Gross - Fees).

## The Platform Fee
To maintain servers, provide customer support, and continuously upgrade features, UniTix charges a standard **2.5% platform fee** on all *paid* ticket sales. 

- This fee is automatically deducted from your Gross Revenue.
- The platform fee for Free Events is always $0.
- *Note: This 2.5% fee is a baseline. Depending on high ticket volumes or exclusive partnerships, this fee may be negotiated with UniTix Management.*

## Requesting a Payout
When your event has concluded, you can withdraw your Net Earnings.
1. Navigate to the **Wallet** tab.
2. Ensure your Society Bank Details (IBAN, Account Name) are securely saved in your Profile settings.
3. Click the **Request Payout** button.

Once requested, UniTix Operations will audit the transactions and process the wire transfer directly to your society's bank account within 3-5 business days. You can track the status of your payout (Pending, Processing, Completed) directly from the Wallet.
