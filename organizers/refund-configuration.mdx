# The Dynamic Refund Policy

As an Organizer, you have total control over whether your event offers refunds, and what penalties apply when a student cancels their ticket.

## How to Configure the Policy
When creating or editing an event, scroll to the **Cancellation & Refund Policy** section.

If you enable refunds, you must configure **Penalty Tiers**. The total percentage across the 3 fields in a tier MUST equal 100%.

### Example Setup
**Tier 1: Cancelled over 7 Days before event**
- `% Refunded to Student`: 80%
- `% Kept by Society`: 10%
- `% Kept by Platform (UniTix)`: 10% (Note: UniTix keeps the baseline fee + processing overhead)

**Tier 2: Cancelled 48 Hours to 7 Days before event**
- `% Refunded to Student`: 50%
- `% Kept by Society`: 30%
- `% Kept by Platform (UniTix)`: 20%

*Hardcoded Rule: Cancellations within 24 hours of purchase are 100% refunded to the student (buyer's remorse), and cancellations under 48 hours before the event are 0% refunded to prevent last-minute empty seats.*

## Automated Processing
When a student clicks "Cancel Reservation" in their dashboard, the platform automatically determines which time window they fall into. 
- The ticket is immediately released to the Waitlist.
- Your Society's Gross Revenue is instantly recalculated based on the penalty percentage you kept.
- UniTix Operations will handle routing the refunded percentage back to the student.
