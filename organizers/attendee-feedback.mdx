    # Attendee Feedback & Post-Event Reviews

    The **Event Feedback Hub** (`/society-dashboard/events/[id]/feedback`) allows organizers to collect verified attendee reviews, track attendee satisfaction, and benchmark future event quality.

    ---

    ## 1. Automated Feedback Collection

    - After an attendee checks in at the venue gate, the platform automatically prompts them to submit an event review through their Student Dashboard.
    - **Verified Reviews**: Only attendees who physically checked in can submit ratings, preventing fake or spam feedback.

    ---

    ## 2. Key Feedback Metrics

    - **Overall Star Rating**: Average 5-star score across all attendee submissions.
    - **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: Percentage of promoters vs detractors, measuring how likely attendees are to recommend your society's future events.
    - **Written Reviews & Suggestions**: Detailed feedback on venue acoustics, speaker quality, food, and organization.
