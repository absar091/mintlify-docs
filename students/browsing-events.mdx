    # Discovering Events on UniTix

    The UniTix marketplace makes it easy to discover what is happening across your campus and sister universities.

    ---

    ## 1. Step-by-Step: Searching & Filtering Events

    ```text
    Step 1: Open Marketplace ➔ Step 2: Apply Filters ➔ Step 3: View Event Details ➔ Step 4: Book Passes
    ```

    1. Go to the **Explore Events** page (`https://unitix.aarx.online` or `/dashboard/events`).
    2. **Search Bar**: Type any keyword (e.g., _AI Workshop, Annual Gala, Battle of the Bands, Model UN_).
    3. **Filter by University**: Select your university campus to view local events, or choose "All Universities" to find national competitions.
    4. **Filter by Category**: Narrow down by _Technical, Cultural, Sports, Academic, or Social_.
    5. **Filter by Society**: View events hosted by specific student bodies (e.g., _ACM, IEEE, Debating Club_).

    ---

    ## 2. Event Cards & Countdown Timers

    Each event card displays essential details at a glance:

    - **Live Countdown Timer**: Days, hours, and minutes remaining until registration closes.
    - **Price Range**: Displays starting price (or _Free_).
    - **Remaining Seats / Capacity Indicator**: Shows if an event is filling up fast.
    - **Society Badge**: Official verified student society badge.
