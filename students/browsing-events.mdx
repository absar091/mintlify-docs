# Finding the Perfect Event

UniTix makes it incredibly simple to discover what's happening at your university.

## The Event Feed
When you land on the **Home Page**, you will see a feed of upcoming events sorted by date. 

**How to filter events:**
1. Use the **Search Bar** at the top to type in specific keywords (e.g., "Hackathon", "Concert").
2. Click the **Category Tags** (e.g., *Tech*, *Entertainment*, *Sports*) to filter the feed.

## Event Details Page
Once you find an event that interests you, click on its card to open the **Public Event Page**. This page serves as a mini-website for the event.

**What you will find here:**
- **Banner & Title:** The main branding of the event.
- **Date & Location:** Exactly when and where the event is happening.
- **Sponsors:** Any corporate partners officially supporting the event.
- **The Ticket Selection Box:** This is where you can view available tiers (e.g., Early Bird vs. General Admission), check prices, and see if an event is Sold Out.

## Society Profiles
If you enjoy an event, you might want to see what else that Society is organizing.
1. Scroll to the bottom of the Event Details page.
2. Click on the **Organized By [Society Name]** badge.
3. This will open the **Society Public Profile**, where you can read their bio and see a list of all their upcoming and past events.
