# Finding the Right Event

UniTix makes it easy to see exactly what is happening on your campus. By default, the homepage showcases **Featured Events** curated for their popularity and scale.

To see everything available, navigate to the **Events** tab. Here you can:
- View all published events.
- See real-time ticket availability (e.g., "Sold Out" or "Selling Fast").
- Identify which society is hosting the event.

# The Checkout Process

When you find an event you want to attend, click on it to view the full details, including the venue, date, sponsors, and available ticket tiers.

## Ticket Tiers
Organizers often create multiple ticket tiers (for example: Early Bird, General Admission, and VIP). Each tier has its own price and availability. Select the tier that best fits your needs.

## Free Events
If the ticket tier is free, simply click "Register". Your digital ticket will be instantly generated and added to your account.

## Paid Events & Payment Verification
For paid events, UniTix supports flexible payment ingest methods depending on the organizer's preferences.

1. **Select Payment Method:** Often, societies will provide direct bank transfer details, JazzCash, or EasyPaisa numbers.
2. **Transfer Funds:** Make the payment using your banking or mobile wallet app.
3. **Upload Proof:** Take a screenshot of your successful transaction and upload it directly in the UniTix checkout portal.
4. **Pending Verification:** Your ticket will initially be marked as "Pending". The society's financial team will manually review your screenshot to verify the funds were received.
5. **Ticket Issued:** Once verified, your digital ticket will unlock!

<Warning>
Do not share your payment screenshot with anyone else. Organizers strictly audit transaction IDs to prevent fraud.
</Warning>
