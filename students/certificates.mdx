# Claiming Your Certificate

Many academic and tech-focused societies offer official Certificates of Attendance for participating in their workshops or summits. UniTix automates this process.

If an organizer has enabled certificates for their event, you can claim yours immediately after the event concludes—**provided you were successfully checked in at the gate.** (If your ticket was never scanned, you cannot claim a certificate).

## How to Generate Your Certificate
1. Log in to your UniTix dashboard.
2. Navigate to the **Certificates** tab in the sidebar.
3. You will see a list of eligible past events you attended.
4. Click **Claim Certificate** next to the event.
5. The platform will automatically generate a high-quality certificate bearing your registered Name and University ID.

## Downloading & Verifying
Once claimed, you have two options:
1. **Download PDF:** Click the Download button to save the certificate as a high-resolution PDF, perfect for printing or attaching to your resume.
2. **Verification Link:** Every certificate generated by UniTix includes a unique ID (e.g., `UTX-TECH-84F2`) and a permanent verification link. 

**Why Verification Matters:**
If you add this certificate to your LinkedIn profile or resume, employers can visit the verification link (e.g., `unitix.aarx.online/verify-certificate`), type in your ID, and our servers will independently confirm that you attended the event, proving the certificate is not forged.
