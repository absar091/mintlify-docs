# Refund Policy

At UniTix, **refunds depend entirely on the Society organizing the event.** 

Because UniTix acts as a ticketing platform connecting you with campus organizers, we do not hold the ticketing funds directly, nor do we dictate refund rules for individual events.

## Society-Specific Rules
When creating an event, the organizing Society selects one of two options:
1. **No Refunds:** If the society selects "No Refunds", all sales are final. UniTix cannot process a refund under any circumstances.
2. **Conditional Refunds:** If the society allows refunds, they will configure a specific policy window (e.g., "Refunds allowed up to 24 hours before the event"). You can request a refund directly through your dashboard if you fall within this window.

## Platform Fees
If a refund is granted by the society, please note that the **UniTix Platform Fee (2.5%) is strictly non-refundable**. You will receive your money back minus this small processing fee.

# Cancellations

If an event is cancelled or significantly postponed by the organizer, the Society is responsible for issuing refunds to all attendees. UniTix will assist the society in processing these bulk refunds, but the funds must be provided by the organizing body.

# Ticket Transfers

To maintain security and prevent unauthorized scalping, **UniTix tickets are strictly non-transferable.**

Your digital ticket is cryptographically tied to your UniTix account and your identity. Event scanners will display your registered name at the gate. If you cannot attend an event, you must cancel your ticket (which will automatically offer it to the next person on the waitlist) rather than attempting to send a screenshot to a friend, as screenshots will fail at the scanning gate.
