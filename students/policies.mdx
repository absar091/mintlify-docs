# The Dynamic Refund Policy

At UniTix, **refunds depend entirely on the Society organizing the event.** 

When an organizer creates an event, they configure a **Cancellation & Refund Policy**. This policy consists of multiple tiers that determine how penalty fees are distributed based on *when* you cancel your ticket.

## How the Penalty Tiers Work
If an event allows refunds, the organizer will define strict time windows. For example:
- **Tier 1 (e.g., Over 7 Days before event):** You might receive an 80% refund. The Society keeps 10% as a penalty, and UniTix keeps 10% to cover processing.
- **Tier 2 (e.g., 48 Hours to 7 Days before event):** You might receive a 50% refund. The Society keeps 30%, and UniTix keeps 20%.
- **Final Window (e.g., Under 48 hours):** Cancellations extremely close to the event are almost always **0% refunded**.

*Note: You can view the specific refund policy for any event on its checkout page before you purchase.*

## How to Cancel a Ticket
If you can no longer attend and wish to trigger the refund policy:
1. Go to your **My Tickets** dashboard.
2. Click on the ticket.
3. Click **Cancel Reservation**.
4. The system will calculate your refund based on the current time window and immediately release your ticket to the Waitlist.
5. If a refund is due, the amount will be credited back to your original payment method (or you will be contacted for bank details) within 5-7 business days.

# Event Cancellations

If an event is cancelled or significantly postponed by the organizer themselves, the Society is legally responsible for issuing full refunds to all attendees. UniTix will assist the society in processing these bulk refunds, but the funds must be provided by the organizing body.

# Ticket Transfers

If you do not want to cancel your ticket, you may use the official **Ticket Transfer** feature to give it to a friend (assuming the organizer has allowed transfers). Please see the [Ticket Transfers](/students/ticket-transfer) guide for step-by-step instructions.

**Screenshots are strictly prohibited.** Your digital ticket is cryptographically tied to your identity. Attempting to send a screenshot to a friend will result in a failed scan at the gate.
