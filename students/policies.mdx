# Transparent Cancellation Policies

At UniTix, cancellation eligibility and penalty deductions are governed by **each organizing society's customized refund policy**. Because societies book physical venues and arrange catering based on ticket numbers, cancellation rules vary depending on how close to event day you submit your request.

---

## 1. How Tiered Cancellation Penalties Work
When an organizer launches an event with refunds enabled, they configure strict time horizons. Before completing your checkout, you can inspect the exact policy on the event page:

### Typical Multi-Tier Penalty Example:
- **Tier 1 (e.g., More than 7 Days before event):** You receive an **80% refund** of your ticket price. The society retains a `10%` penalty cut, and UniTix retains `10%` for gateway reversal costs.
- **Tier 2 (e.g., 48 Hours to 7 Days before event):** You receive a **50% refund**. The society retains `30%`, and UniTix retains `20%`.
- **Final Window (e.g., Under 48 hours):** Cancellations submitted within 48 hours of event commencement receive a **0% refund**.

---

## 2. Universal Platform Protections
Regardless of individual society settings, all student checkouts are protected by two universal platform mandates:

### 🛡️ 24-Hour Buyer's Remorse Guarantee
If you accidentally purchase the wrong ticket tier or date and click *Cancel Reservation* **within 24 hours of your purchase timestamp** (and the event is more than 48 hours away), you are automatically granted a **100% full refund** without penalty deductions.

### 🛑 48-Hour Final Lock
Once an event is less than 48 hours away, automated cancellation requests are locked across all standard tickets (`0% refund`). This ensures event organizers do not suffer sudden financial deficits or empty seating during final logistics prep.

---

## 3. How to Request a Cancellation & Refund
If your plans change and your ticket is within an eligible refund window:
1. Log into **My Tickets** (`unitix.aarx.online`).
2. Click on the ticket card you wish to cancel.
3. Click **Cancel Reservation**.
4. A confirmation modal displays the exact refund calculation (`PKR`) based on the current time window.
5. Confirm the cancellation. Your ticket is immediately voided (`cancelled`), and the freed inventory slot is automatically transferred to the waitlist.
6. **Processing Timeline:** Eligible refund amounts (`PKR`) are credited back to your original payment account or processed via direct bank wire within **5 to 7 business days**.

---

## 4. Organizer-Initiated Event Cancellations
If a society postpones or completely cancels an event:
- **100% Mandatory Refund:** The organizing society is legally and contractually bound to issue full, 100% reimbursement to all confirmed ticket holders.
- UniTix Operations manages the bulk refund distribution, ensuring every student receives their full payment back without any penalty deductions.
