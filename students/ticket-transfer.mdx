# Giving Your Ticket to Someone Else

While you cannot simply screenshot your ticket to give it to a friend, UniTix provides an official, secure **Ticket Transfer** feature. This ensures the new owner receives a valid, cryptographically signed QR code under their own name.

> [!WARNING]
> **Check Event Rules:** Some high-security events may have transfers disabled by the organizers. If the "Transfer Ticket" button is greyed out, the society does not permit transfers for that specific event.

## How to Initiate a Transfer
If you can no longer attend an event and want to give your ticket to someone else:

1. Log in to UniTix and navigate to the **My Tickets** dashboard.
2. Find the ticket you wish to transfer.
3. Click the **Transfer Ticket** button (represented by a 'Send' icon) located on the ticket card.
4. The system will process the request and generate a unique, one-time **Transfer Link**.
5. Click to **Copy** the link to your clipboard.
6. Send this link to the recipient via WhatsApp, email, or any messaging app.

## How to Claim a Transferred Ticket
If a friend sent you a Transfer Link:

1. Click the link provided by your friend (e.g., `unitix.aarx.online/transfer/abc-123`).
2. You will be prompted to **Log In** or **Sign Up** for a Student Account. (You must have an account to claim a ticket).
3. Once logged in, you will see a summary of the ticket (Event Name, Tier, Date).
4. Click **Claim Transfer**.
5. The system will securely invalidate the sender's QR code and generate a brand new one attached to your account.
6. The ticket will now appear in your **My Tickets** dashboard!
