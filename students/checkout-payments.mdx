# Purchasing a Ticket

Once you've selected your desired ticket tier on the Event Page, it's time to check out. UniTix supports both Free and Paid events.

## Claiming a Free Ticket
If the event is completely free:
1. Select the quantity (usually limited to 1 per student).
2. Click **Get Free Ticket**.
3. You will be instantly redirected to a Success page, and your ticket will appear in your **My Tickets** dashboard. No payment info required!

## Paying for a Ticket
If the ticket has a cost, UniTix utilizes a secure **Pending Payments** system to support local transaction methods like JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or direct Bank Transfers.

**How to complete a paid checkout:**
1. Click **Buy Ticket** on your selected tier.
2. You will be taken to the Checkout screen. Here, you will see the Society's official bank details (Account Name and IBAN).
3. **Open your banking app** (or JazzCash/EasyPaisa) on your phone.
4. Transfer the exact ticket amount to the provided bank details.
5. Take a screenshot of the successful transaction receipt.
6. Return to UniTix. Enter the **Transaction ID** from your receipt into the input field.
7. Click **Upload Receipt** and attach your screenshot.
8. Click **Submit Payment**.

## The Verification Process
Your ticket will now appear in your dashboard with a yellow **PENDING** status.

The Society Organizers receive a notification and will manually verify your screenshot against their bank statement. 
- If valid, they will **Approve** it, and your ticket will turn green (Valid).
- If invalid or the amount is incorrect, they will **Reject** it, and your ticket reservation will be canceled. You will receive an email notification in both cases.
