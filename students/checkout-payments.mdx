# Secure Ticket Checkout

Once you select your preferred ticket tier on an Event Page, you enter the **UniTix Checkout Flow**. Our checkout engine supports instant free claims as well as localized manual bank and mobile wallet verifications (`JazzCash`, `EasyPaisa`, `IBFT`).

---

## 1. Claiming Free Tickets
If the event or ticket tier is priced at `Rs. 0` (Free):
1. Select your desired quantity (subject to per-student purchase limits set by the organizer).
2. Click **Get Free Ticket**.
3. The system instantly registers your attendance without requesting any financial or bank details.
4. You are routed directly to the **Success Confirmation Page**, and your digital QR pass (`qrToken`) is immediately deposited into your **My Tickets** wallet.

---

## 2. Paying for Tickets via Manual Bank / JazzCash
Because many university students do not possess active credit/debit cards, UniTix utilizes a localized **Manual Receipt Verification Engine** (`pending_payment`).

### Step-by-Step Paid Checkout Workflow:
1. Click **Buy Ticket** on your selected paid tier (e.g., `General Admission - Rs. 500`).
2. On the checkout screen, review the **Official Society Bank Details**:
   - **Bank / Wallet Name:** e.g., *JazzCash*, *EasyPaisa*, or *HBL Bank*
   - **Account Title:** e.g., *UniTix Society Ledger* or *ACM Chapter*
   - **Account / IBAN Number:** e.g., `PK99JAZZ00001234567890`
3. **Execute Transfer:** Open your personal banking or mobile wallet app (`JazzCash`, `EasyPaisa`, `Sadapay`, `NayaPay`) and transfer the **exact ticket amount (`PKR`)** to the society's account.
4. **Capture Screenshot:** Take a clear screenshot of your successful transaction receipt showing the **Date, Amount, and Transaction Reference ID (TxID)**.
5. **Submit Receipt on UniTix:**
   - Enter your exact **Transaction Reference ID / TxID** into the text input field.
   - Click **Upload Screenshot** and select your saved payment receipt.
   - Click **Submit Payment for Verification**.

---

## 3. The Verification Review State (`Pending Payment`)
Once submitted, your order transitions to the **Pending Payment (`pending_payment`)** state inside your **My Tickets** dashboard:
- Your inventory slot is temporarily reserved while the society review team verifies your receipt against their bank ledger.
- **Approval:** Once the organizer verifies the funds arrived in their bank account and clicks *Approve*, your ticket instantly turns green (`Approved / Valid`) and your digital QR code unlocks. You will receive an instant email confirmation.
- **Rejection:** If your screenshot is illegible, duplicate, or the transaction ID cannot be found in the society bank statement, the organizer will click *Reject*. Your ticket reservation will be voided (`Cancelled`) and the inventory slot released. You will receive a rejection notification via email with instructions on how to retry or contact support.

---

## 4. Cancelling a Pending Reservation
If you submitted a payment receipt by mistake or decided not to attend before the organizer approves your ticket:
1. Go to **My Tickets**.
2. Click on the pending ticket card.
3. Click **Cancel Reservation**.
4. Your pending reservation is immediately cancelled (`cancelled`), and your slot is released to the waitlist.
