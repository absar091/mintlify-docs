# Waitlist Automation

Highly anticipated campus events often sell out in minutes. Instead of refreshing the page endlessly or buying tickets from scalpers, UniTix features an **Automated Waitlist Engine**.

## Joining the Waitlist
When a ticket tier sells out, the "Buy" button is replaced with a "Join Waitlist" button. 
Clicking this adds you to a chronological queue for that specific ticket tier.

## How Tickets are Re-Allocated
Tickets become available if:
1. Another student cancels their ticket.
2. The society increases the venue capacity.
3. A pending payment is rejected by the organizer.

When a ticket becomes available, the Waitlist Engine automatically reserves it for the **first person** in the queue.

## Claiming Your Reservation
If a ticket is reserved for you, you will receive an immediate email notification.
- **The 24-Hour Window:** You have exactly 24 hours to claim and pay for your reserved ticket.
- **Automatic Expiration:** If you do not claim the ticket within 24 hours, your reservation expires, and the ticket is automatically offered to the next person in line. 

<Tip>
Make sure emails from `no-reply@unitix.aarx.online` are not going to your spam folder so you don't miss your reservation window!
</Tip>
