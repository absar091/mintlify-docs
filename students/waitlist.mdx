# Joining the Waitlist

When an event reaches its maximum venue capacity, or a specific ticket tier sells out, ticket sales are immediately halted. Instead of turning you away, UniTix automatically activates the Waitlist system.

**How to join:**
1. Navigate to the sold-out Event Page.
2. The "Buy Ticket" button will have changed to **Join Waitlist**.
3. Click it to reserve your spot in line. You do not need to enter payment info to join the waitlist.

## Claiming a Waitlist Spot

If an attendee cancels their ticket, or the organizers increase the venue capacity, the system will automatically release a ticket to the *first* person on the waitlist.

**How it works:**
1. You will receive an **email notification** that a ticket has become available for you.
2. The system gives you a strict **24-hour window** to claim the ticket.
3. Log in to UniTix, go to your Dashboard, and you will see an alert prompting you to complete your purchase.
4. If you fail to complete the checkout within 24 hours, the reservation expires, and the ticket is immediately offered to the next person in line.
