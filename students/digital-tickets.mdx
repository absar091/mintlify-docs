# Your Digital QR Pass

UniTix eliminates paper tickets and physical printouts. Once your ticket purchase or free claim is confirmed, your smartphone becomes your official pass to the event.

---

## 1. Accessing Your Tickets
1. Log into your **Student Dashboard** (`unitix.aarx.online`).
2. Click **My Tickets** in the top navigation bar.
3. You will see a grid of all your active, pending, and past event passes.
4. Click on any active card to expand the **Full-Screen Digital Pass**, which displays:
   - **Hero Visual & Event Title**
   - **Verified Student Full Name & Roll Number**
   - **Assigned Ticket Tier (`VIP`, `Early Bird`, `General Admission`)**
   - **High-Contrast Cryptographic QR Code (`qrToken`)**
   - **Live Check-in Status Badge**

---

## 2. Cryptographic Security & Screenshot Blocking
Unlike standard static QR codes that simply contain a website link or random text, UniTix digital passes generate a dynamic, cryptographically signed token (`qrToken`).

### Why Screenshots Are Strictly Prohibited
To protect campus events from duplicate entries and unauthorized badge sharing:
- **Instant Use Lock:** When the Gate Scanner reads your QR pass at the entrance, your ticket status in the global database instantly flips from `Approved` to `Used`.
- **Double-Scan Rejection:** If a classmate or friend attempts to scan a saved screenshot of your QR code even 30 seconds later, the scanner triggers a loud security alarm displaying: `Warning: Already Scanned Today (Time: XX:XX)`.
- **Offline & Low-Light Presentation:** If your venue entrance has poor cellular reception, pre-load your **My Tickets** page while on campus Wi-Fi. The QR pass remains fully scannable from browser cache, and our high-contrast rendering ensures fast reads even on dim screens.

## 3. Understanding Ticket Status Badges

| Status Badge | Visual Indicator | Exact Status Meaning |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **🟢 APPROVED / VALID** | Green Badge | Ticket is confirmed, paid/verified, and ready for gate check-in. |
| **🟡 PENDING PAYMENT** | Amber Badge | Your manual payment receipt was uploaded and is awaiting organizer verification. |
| **🔵 TRANSFER PENDING** | Blue Badge | You initiated a transfer link. Your QR code is locked until the recipient claims or you cancel the transfer. |
| **🟣 USED / CHECKED IN** | Purple Badge | Your QR pass was successfully scanned at the venue gate. |
| **🔴 CANCELLED / REFUNDED** | Red Badge | Your ticket was voided, refunded, or rejected by the organizer. QR is permanently revoked. |
