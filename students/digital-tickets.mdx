# Your Digital Ticket

UniTix eliminates the need for paper tickets. Your ticket is a secure, digital pass stored directly in your account.

## Accessing Your Ticket
1. Log in to your UniTix account.
2. Navigate to **My Tickets** in your dashboard.
3. Click on the upcoming event to view your digital pass.

## Security Features

UniTix tickets are incredibly secure. We use **JWT (JSON Web Token) encryption** to generate the QR code on your ticket. 

What this means for you:
- **Screenshot-Proof:** Your QR code regenerates and is cryptographically signed. If someone tries to use a screenshot of your ticket, the scanner will immediately flag it as invalid.
- **Unique Identification:** Every ticket is tied to your account and a unique alphanumeric code (e.g., `UTX-2027-000001`).

## At the Gate
When you arrive at the event:
1. Open your UniTix digital pass on your phone and ensure your screen brightness is turned up.
2. The event staff will scan your QR code using the UniTix Gate Scanner app.
3. The scan takes less than 2 seconds, and you will be checked in immediately.

<Tip>
Because the tickets require live verification, please make sure you load your ticket while you have internet access before reaching the gate.
</Tip>
