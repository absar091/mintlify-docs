# Your Cryptographic Ticket

UniTix does not use easily-faked PDF attachments or static images. Every ticket is a dynamic, cryptographically signed JSON Web Token (JWT) represented as a QR code.

## Accessing Your Ticket
1. Log in to your UniTix account on your smartphone.
2. Click **My Tickets** in the top navigation bar.
3. You will see a list of all your upcoming and past events. Click on an upcoming event to view the ticket.
4. Your unique **QR Code** will be displayed in the center of the screen, along with your Name, University ID, and the Ticket Tier (e.g., VIP).

## At the Gate
**How to check in:**
1. Open the My Tickets page on your phone while waiting in line.
2. Turn up your screen brightness.
3. Present the live QR code to the Gate Scanner volunteer.
4. They will scan it, and their device will instantly verify the cryptographic signature and check you into the system.

> [!WARNING]
> **Do NOT take a screenshot!** The Gate Scanner app tracks live validation. If you attempt to send a screenshot of your ticket to a friend, or present a screenshot at the gate, the scanner will flag it as a duplicate or invalid ticket, and you may be denied entry.
