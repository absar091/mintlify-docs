# Sponsorship Management

University societies rely heavily on corporate sponsorships (e.g., RedBull, local restaurants, tech companies) to fund their events.

Sponsors want to know that their logo is actually being seen by students. UniTix provides a dedicated sponsorship engine to facilitate this.

## The Society Rolodex

Instead of uploading sponsor logos over and over for every event, Society Admins manage a central "Rolodex" of sponsors in their dashboard.

You can add a sponsor with:
- Their company name.
- Their high-resolution Logo.
- Their website link.
- Their Tier (Platinum, Gold, Silver).

## Attaching to Events

When creating or editing an event, the Society Admin can simply select sponsors from their Rolodex to attach them to that specific event.

These sponsors automatically appear:
1. On the public-facing **Event Details** page, providing SEO and click-through value to the sponsor.
2. At the bottom of the **Digital Ticket Pass**, ensuring that every single attendee sees the sponsor's logo when they pull out their phone at the gate!

This measurable visibility makes it significantly easier for societies to secure funding for future events.
