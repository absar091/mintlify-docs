    # Comprehensive Getting Started Guide

    This onboarding guide provides clear, step-by-step instructions for university students, society leadership, and event operations teams to configure their profiles, verify academic credentials, and utilize the UniTix platform.

    ---

    ## 1. Student Onboarding & Academic Verification

    ```
    Step 1: Sign Up ➔ Step 2: Verify Email ➔ Step 3: Complete Campus Profile ➔ Step 4: Access Student Marketplace
    ```

    ### Step 1: Create Your Student Account
    1. Open `https://unitix.aarx.online/signup` in your browser.
    2. Enter your primary university email address (or personal email) and create a password with at least 8 characters.
    3. Click **Create Account**.

    ### Step 2: Verify Your Email Address
    1. Check your email inbox for the verification link.
    2. Click the verification link to confirm your email and activate your session.

    ### Step 3: Complete Academic Campus Profile (`/dashboard/profile`)
    1. Go to **Profile** in the top navigation bar.
    2. Enter your **Full Legal Name** (exactly as registered on your official university ID card).
    3. Enter your **Registration / Roll Number** (e.g., `2022-CS-101` or `21-EE-045`). This number is permanently stamped onto your dynamic QR passes and verified attendance certificates.
    4. Select your **Department & University Campus**.
    5. Upload a clear portrait photo. When you present your pass at the gate, this picture assists security staff in confirming your physical identity.
    6. Click **Save Profile**.

    ### Step 4: Earning the Verified Campus Student Badge
    - Accounts registered with official institutional emails or verified roll numbers automatically receive the **Verified Campus Student** badge.
    - **Benefits**: Direct access to campus-restricted events, student-only tier pricing, fast-track gate entry, and claimable certificates.

    ---

    ## 2. Society President & Executive Onboarding

    ```
    Step 1: Student Account ➔ Step 2: Submit Society Application ➔ Step 3: Faculty Advisor Endorsement ➔ Step 4: Dashboard Access
    ```

    ### Step 1: Submit Society Registration Application
    1. Log into your verified student account on `https://unitix.aarx.online`.
    2. Click **Register Society** in the navigation menu.
    3. Complete the **Multi-Section Society Application**:
       - **Society Identity**: Official Society Name, University Campus, short URL handle (e.g., `/s/acm-chapter`), 1:1 Square Logo, and 16:9 Banner.
       - **Faculty Advisor Information**: Official Name, Designation, and university email address (`@university.edu.pk`) of your supervising Professor.
       - **Official Society Banking Details**: Bank Name, Account Title, and 24-character IBAN. Direct wire disbursements (`IBFT`) are routed exclusively to this account.
    4. Click **Submit Application for Verification**.

    ### Step 2: The Verification & Review Workflow
    - **Administrative Compliance Audit**: Operations staff review the society application, verify university affiliation, and validate bank details within 24 to 48 hours.
    - **Faculty Advisor Endorsement**: An automated email is sent to your Faculty Advisor. Once they click to endorse supervision, your society receives the **Verified Society Badge**.
    - **Executive Dashboard Access**: Upon approval, your navigation bar immediately unlocks the **Society Dashboard** with access to the 8-Step Event Builder, 5-Tab Marketing Hub, Gate Scanner APK, and Wallet!

    ---

    ## 3. Volunteer Gate Scanner Staff Onboarding

    ```
    Step 1: Receive Staff Credentials ➔ Step 2: Install Scanner App ➔ Step 3: Download Manifest ➔ Step 4: Begin Gate Scanning
    ```

    1. **Receive Credentials**: The society executive creates your scanner account and assigns you to the event.
    2. **Access Scanner**:
       - **Android Devices**: Install the native `unitix_scanner.apk`.
       - **Other Devices / Laptops**: Open the Web App at `https://unitix.aarx.online/scan`.
    3. **Log in**: Enter your assigned scanner username and password.
    4. **Select Event**: Tap the assigned event card on your screen.
    5. **Download Manifest (If scanning offline)**: Tap the **Download Manifest** button while connected to Wi-Fi to cache all attendee passes before entering the hall.
    6. **Start Gate Scanning**: Tap **Start Gate Scan** to begin admitting attendees!

    ---

    ## 4. Quick Action Reference Checklist

    | Action | Portal Path | What It Accomplishes |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | **Discover Campus Events** | `/dashboard/events` | Search upcoming events, filter by campus, and select ticket tiers. |
    | **View & Present Passes** | `/dashboard/tickets` | Display dynamic rotating QR pass with screenshot protection. |
    | **Transfer a Ticket** | `/dashboard/tickets/[id]` | Generate secure claim link to transfer a ticket to a friend. |
    | **Download Invoices** | `/dashboard/financials` | Review spending ledger and download PDF payment receipts. |
    | **Build a New Event** | `/dashboard/events/create` | Follow the 8-step wizard to configure tiers, Spotify music, and capacity stops. |
    | **Launch Promo Codes** | `/dashboard/events/[id]/marketing` | Create discount codes, generate posters, and send email broadcasts. |
    | **Pair Gate Phones** | Scanner App $\to$ Event Hub | Connect multiple scanner phones via wireless mesh without internet. |
    | **Request Bank Payout** | `/society-dashboard/wallet` | Check available balance and request direct wire transfer to your bank. |

    # آغاز کا مکمل اور تفصیلی طریقہ کار

    یہ گائیڈ طلباء، سوسائٹی صدور اور گیٹ کے عملے کو اکاؤنٹ بنانے، تعلیمی تصدیق اور سسٹم استعمال کرنے کا مکمل طریقہ سکھاتی ہے:

    ---

    ## 1. طالب علم کا اکاؤنٹ اور کیمپس تصدیق

    1. **اکاؤنٹ بنائیں**: `https://unitix.aarx.online/signup` پر جا کر یونیورسٹی ای میل اور پاس ورڈ درج کریں۔
    2. **ای میل تصدیق**: ان باکس میں آئے تصدیقی لنک پر کلک کریں۔
    3. **پروفائل مکمل کریں (`/dashboard/profile`)**:
       - پورا نام (جو طالب علم کارڈ پر درج ہے)۔
       - یونیورسٹی کا رول نمبر (مثلاً `2022-CS-101`)۔ یہ نمبر ٹکٹ اور سرٹیفکیٹ پر درج ہوگا۔
       - شعبہ اور کیمپس کا نام۔
       - اپنی واضح تصویر اپ لوڈ کریں تاکہ گیٹ پر شناخت میں آسانی ہو۔
    4. **تصدیق شدہ بیج**: یونیورسٹی ای میل کی تصدیق پر **Verified Student** کا بیج دیا جائے گا جس سے خصوصی سستے ٹکٹ ملیں گے۔

    ---

    ## 2. سوسائٹی صدر کے لیے رجسٹریشن کا طریقہ

    1. اپنے اسٹوڈنٹ اکاؤنٹ میں لاگ ان کریں۔
    2. اوپر مینو میں **Register Society** پر جائیں۔
    3. فارم پر سوسائٹی کا نام، کیمپس، لوگو اور بینر درج کریں۔
    4. نگران پروفیسر (Faculty Advisor) کا نام اور سرکاری ای میل درج کریں۔
    5. سوسائٹی کے بینک اکاؤنٹ کا نام اور 24 ہندسوں پر مشتمل IBAN درج کریں۔
    6. پروفیسر اور ایڈمن کی تصدیق کے بعد **Society Dashboard** کا مکمل کنٹرول مل جائے گا!

    ---

    ## 3. گیٹ سکینر عملے کے لیے ہدایات

    1. سوسائٹی انتظامیہ سے اپنا لاگ ان پاس ورڈ حاصل کریں۔
    2. موبائل پر سکینر ایپ (Android APK) کھولیں یا براؤزر میں `unitix.aarx.online/scan` پر جائیں۔
    3. ایونٹ کارڈ پر **Download Manifest** کا بٹن دبا کر تمام ٹکٹ موبائل میں محفوظ کریں۔
    4. **Start Gate Scan** پر کلک کر کے طلباء کے ٹکٹ سکین کرنا شروع کریں!
