# Creating an Account

Getting started on UniTix is fast and secure. We require all users to create an account to ensure a safe, authenticated environment for campus ticketing.

## Student Accounts

If you just want to attend events, a Student account is all you need.

1. Navigate to the **Sign Up** page.
2. Enter your Name, Email, and a secure password.
3. You will receive an email verification link. **You must verify your email** before you can purchase or reserve tickets.
4. Once verified, you can browse all events, join waitlists, and access your digital tickets.

## Registering a Society

If you represent a student organization and want to host events on UniTix, you must submit a Society Registration request. To maintain platform integrity, all societies undergo a strict approval process by UniTix Management.

### What You Need

Before you begin the registration process, please ensure you have the following information ready:

- **Society Name & Description:** A clear, accurate representation of your organization.
- **University Affiliation:** Select the university your society operates within.
- **Faculty Advisor Information:** 
  - You must provide the **Name** and **Contact Information (Email/Phone)** of your official university faculty advisor.
  - UniTix Management may contact your advisor to verify the legitimacy of your society before granting publishing rights.

### The Approval Process

1. Submit your registration form via the **Register Society** portal.
2. Verify your email address. **Your application will not be reviewed until your email is verified.**
3. UniTix Management will review your application and verify your details (including contacting your faculty advisor if necessary).
4. Upon approval, your account will automatically be upgraded to a **Society Admin**, granting you access to the Organizer Dashboard.

<Note>
Approval times typically range from 24-48 business hours. You will receive an email notification as soon as a decision is made.
</Note>
