# Creating a Student Account

To purchase tickets, transfer them, or join waitlists, you need a Student Account.

**How to Sign Up:**
1. Navigate to `unitix.aarx.online` and click **Login / Sign Up** in the top right.
2. Enter your primary email address and a secure password.
3. Verify your email address (check your inbox for a confirmation link).
4. Complete your profile by entering your **Full Name** and **University Roll/Registration Number**.
5. Upload a clear **Profile Picture**. *(Important: This picture is displayed to Gate Scanners to verify your identity. Use a clear headshot.)*

# Registering a Society (For Organizers)

If you are a Society President or Executive, you must submit your society for approval before you can start selling tickets.

**How to Register Your Society:**
1. Create a standard Student Account (see above).
2. While logged in, click **Register Society** in the navigation bar.
3. Fill out the multi-step application form:
   - **Basic Details:** Society Name, University Name, and a Logo.
   - **Faculty Advisor:** Provide the Name, Email, and Phone Number of the university staff member overseeing your society.
   - **President Details:** Provide your CNIC (National ID Card) Front and Back images.
   - **Bank Details:** Enter the official IBAN and Account Title where ticket payouts should be routed.
4. Click **Submit Application**.

**What Happens Next?**
Your application enters the **Pending** state. The UniTix Operations team will manually review your submitted documents to ensure compliance with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations and verify your society's authenticity. This process usually takes 24-48 hours. Once approved, your dashboard will unlock!
