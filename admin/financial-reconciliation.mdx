# Financial Audit & Payout Operations

The **Financial Reconciliation Hub** (`admin-dashboard/payouts` and `admin-dashboard/refunds`) provides root platform operators with absolute oversight over the entire financial ledger. Every ticket sale, cancellation deduction, and payout request flows through immutable transaction records.

---

## 1. Processing Society Payout Requests
When an event concludes and a society requests a withdrawal from their **Available Balance (`PKR`)**, the request appears in the **Pending Payouts Queue** (`/admin-dashboard/payouts`).

### Step-by-Step Payout Execution:
1. Navigate to **Pending Payouts**.
2. **Review Reconciliation Summary:** Inspect the society's total Gross Revenue, total Platform Fee deductions (`2.5%` or `customFeePercentage`), total processed refunds, and any penalty deductions applied by administration.
3. **Verify Bank Ledger:** Check the society's registered Bank Account Title, Bank Name, and International Bank Account Number (`IBAN`).
4. **Execute Wire Transfer (`IBFT / EFT`):** Open the platform's corporate banking portal and execute an electronic funds transfer matching the exact requested take-home amount (`PKR`).
5. **Mark as Paid (`/api/admin/payouts/process`):**
   - Enter the official Bank Transaction Reference Number (`EFT-Ref-UUID`) into the admin dashboard modal.
   - Click **Complete Payout**.
   - The society's wallet balance resets cleanly, and an official payout receipt is deposited directly into their ledger history.

---

## 2. Bulk Refund Reconciliation (`/api/admin/refunds/bulk-complete`)
When an event is postponed or cancelled, or when dozens of students cancel reservations within eligible time windows, those refunds enter the **Refunds Queue** (`/admin-dashboard/refunds`).

### How Bulk Refunds Work:
1. Filter open refund requests by specific event or organizing society.
2. Select individual or bulk student refund rows.
3. **Execute Reversals:** Once funds are reversed via payment gateway or manual bank credit, click **Mark Complete / Bulk Complete**.
4. The ticket documents permanently transition to `status: refunded`, and the accounting ledgers for both the student and the society balance to exact zero.

---

## 3. Managing the University Directory
To ensure structured onboarding across campuses, the Super Admin maintains the **Universities Directory** (`admin-dashboard/universities`).
- Add new target universities along with their official domain extensions (`@lums.edu.pk`, `@nust.edu.pk`, `@giki.edu.pk`).
- Organizers registering from those campuses are automatically mapped to their respective university directories across the student marketplace filters.

---

## 4. User Moderation & Role Assignment
The **Users Management Table** (`admin-dashboard/users`) allows administrators to inspect every registered user account across the entire ecosystem.
- Search users by Name, Roll Number, or Email address.
- **Role Assignment (`/api/admin/users/action`):** Elevate standard students to *Super Admin* or demote compromised accounts.
- **Account Suspension:** Immediately lock user profiles or revoke compromised authentication sessions if suspicious ticket transfer patterns or scalping attempts are detected.
