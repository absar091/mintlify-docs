# The Super Admin Dashboard

The **UniTix Super Admin Control Center** (`/admin-dashboard`) is the dedicated governance portal restricted exclusively to root platform operators and compliance executives. From this central hub, administrators audit society registrations, enforce anti-money laundering (`AML`) checks, assign custom platform fee percentages, and broadcast system-wide alerts.

---

## 1. Society Verification & KYC Auditing
When a university society submits a registration application, their profile enters a `pending` state in the **Societies Directory** (`admin-dashboard/societies`).

### The Verification Workflow:
1. Navigate to **Pending Societies**.
2. **Review KYC Identifiers:** Inspect the submitted **President CNIC (Front & Back)** images, verify the university faculty advisor email (`@university.edu.pk`), and check the official bank account (`IBAN`).
3. **Take Governance Action:**
   - Click **Approve Society:** Immediately changes society status to `active`, unlocks their `Society Dashboard`, and triggers an approval email notification.
   - Click **Reject / Suspend Society:** Blocks society access and suspends all public event ticket sales instantly.

---

## 2. Custom Fee Percentage & Penalty Management
While UniTix applies a global default platform fee of `2.5%` (`platform_settings/global`) to all ticket transactions, the Super Admin can assign custom fee overrides and financial penalties to specific societies (`/api/admin/societies/[id]/fee` and `/api/admin/societies/[id]/penalty`).

### Custom Fee Overrides:
- If a society is a non-profit university charity or has negotiated an institutional agreement, the Super Admin can set their **Custom Platform Fee Percentage (`customFeePercentage`)** to `0%`, `1.0%`, or any custom rate.
- This override dynamically changes the society's **Net Revenue** calculation across all their active events instantly without altering global defaults.

### Financial Penalty & Infraction Enforcement:
- If a society violates safety capacities, cancels events without notice, or submits false payment approvals, the Super Admin can issue a **Financial Penalty Deduction (`PKR`)** directly from their ledger (`admin-dashboard/societies/[id]`).
- The penalty is logged directly in the immutable `audit_logs` collection and subtracted from the society's **Available to Withdraw** balance.

---

## 3. Global Platform Settings (`platform_settings/global`)
The **Settings Panel** (`admin-dashboard/settings`) gives root operators real-time control over system defaults:
- **Global Platform Fee (`%`):** Adjust the universal fee percentage applied across all non-overridden societies.
- **Global Maintenance & Announcement Banner:** Enable top-bar emergency maintenance banners or system announcements displayed to all active student and organizer sessions.
- **Automated Waitlist Cron Configuration:** Adjust waitlist claim windows (`24 hours`) and automatic reallocation intervals.

---

## 4. System-Wide Broadcast Notifications
To communicate urgent platform updates, feature releases, or server maintenance windows, Super Admins can dispatch **System-Wide Broadcasts** (`admin-dashboard/notifications`).
- Select your target audience (`All Students`, `All Organizers`, `Specific University`).
- Enter title, priority (`Normal / High / Urgent`), and rich markdown message.
- Notifications are instantly pushed to user dashboard alert bells and dispatched via automated email summaries.
