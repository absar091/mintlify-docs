# Welcome to UniTix

**UniTix** is a hyper-modern, highly secure event management and ticketing ecosystem designed exclusively to solve the unique challenges of university campus events. 

Built using cutting-edge technologies like **Next.js**, **Firebase**, and **Tailwind CSS**, UniTix replaces outdated paper ticketing systems, manual excel sheets, and chaotic check-in queues with an automated, role-based platform.

## The Problem We Solve
Historically, university societies have struggled with:
1. **Ticket Fraud:** Students sharing screenshots of QR codes to sneak friends in.
2. **Ghosting / No Shows:** Students buying tickets and not showing up, while others desperately want to attend.
3. **Manual Chaos:** Event managers cross-referencing printed lists of thousands of students at the gate.
4. **Sponsor Visibility:** Failing to provide metrics to corporate sponsors about how many students actually saw their branding.

## The UniTix Solution

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="JWT Security" icon="shield-check">
    QR codes are generated using cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT). This makes it mathematically impossible to forge or fake a ticket.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automated Waitlists" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    When an event sells out, an AI-driven cron job manages a rolling waitlist, automatically granting 24-hour reservations as people cancel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zero-Scroll Scanning" icon="qrcode">
    A mobile-first, zero-scroll web scanner built directly into the platform allows staff to scan hundreds of tickets per minute with haptic feedback.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sponsor Portals" icon="handshake">
    Dedicated sponsor rolodexes and event linking ensure that corporate sponsors get maximum visibility on every digital ticket.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
